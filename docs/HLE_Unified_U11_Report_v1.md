## What now works

Groups can contain other groups, people, material objects and finite representations of their organization. Membership may overlap. Every group names its boundary, resources, coordinator and dependencies; summaries have stable identities, exact versions and declared scopes. The same physical resource is counted once even when several subgroups reference it. Ownership and custody remain with their actual holders.

A group can retain a procedure it has demonstrated, and a parent group can call that procedure. The call still requires constituent performances, current consent, applicable resources and each worker's own acquired primitive skills. Joining grants no personal competence. Invitations, acceptance, departure, withdrawal, performed duties and retained collective capacity have separate records.

## Demonstrated circuit

The repair subgroup first performs and observes a repair, then retains that collective capacity. A paid damage operation creates a new need. The parent group calls the subgroup's retained repair procedure and coordinates **repair → transfer → use → care**. Alice performs the repair and transfer; Bob performs the use and care. Each accepts its assigned work independently, pays for its actions, receives the results and processes the observations.

The parent retains its capacity only after all four performances succeed and its coordinator has processed their results. A separately executed detailed program spells out the same primitive leaves. It reaches identical material revisions, primitive work totals and final inventory summaries. The two representations can have different coordination costs; this comparison does not claim a performance improvement.

In the withdrawal witness, the repair has already happened when Bob withdraws consent for the remaining work. An already-paid transfer is refused at commitment. The repair, consumption, spent transfer work and Bob's unfinished duty remain. His later departure removes membership without deleting that obligation. A separate newcomer case permits joining and reading group procedures while continuing to reject unpracticed individual work.

## Scope and limits

This is a bounded contract for native acyclic groups and finite sequential workshop procedures. Ordinary references can represent the containing organization without creating recursive copies. The current linking rule requires a common coordinator and compatible context/cue. Coordinator succession and participant-generated institutions remain U12 work.

The harness supplies group boundaries, invitations, candidate programs, practice, disclosures and opportunities. The engine performs paid coordination, validates consent and membership, executes physical work, advances duties through observations and retains demonstrated collective capacity. Autonomous invention of an institution or collective curriculum is not claimed.

An inventory summary reports unique resources and quantities by actual owner/unit, with unconsumed stock also separated by purpose. Stored stock quantity is its lifetime allocation; current availability is reported separately. A membership summary reports terminal identities and nested groups. Neither is a complete material model or an executable permission. Paid aggregate inspection and explicit delivery/read are separate from internal cache invalidation. Physical selection still requires received detail and actual personal acquisition.

Programs bind concrete participant, resource and procedure identities. Reuse requires renewed run consent and all applicable prerequisites; it does not establish arbitrary aggregate equivalence, universal conceptual growth or generalized individual mastery. The U14 reserved combinations and seeds were not used. Older U1/R21B panels beyond the inherited test selection remain historical evidence. U13 efficiency, U14 release, broader psychological validity and R21C acceptance remain open.

The evidence retains the development failures: a missing native coordination receipt found by the first audit, a missing test-helper import, and a missing witness-exporter import. It also records the lineage compatibility correction and later boundary controls. The final source was frozen before its complete reproduction; earlier passing development runs are not substituted for the final results.
