# C6: situated participant selection and transfer

This is an engineering extension of C5. Formal Crux routes, polarities and inherited destination semantics are unchanged.

A `SelectionRequest` supplies an actor, context, cue, referent, an owned demand binding, accessible resource/partner anchors and search bounds. It accepts no route name, recipe, destination instruction or polarity. The demand is paid retained content with four outcome priorities (personal, material, shared, system), commitment urgency, condition/quantity scope, amount and collective-accountability importance. These initial needs are supplied conditions, not an implemented account of spontaneous desire formation.

`SelectionEngine.selection_view` detaches only paid actor-owned meanings, received message payloads, actual received observations, current received material fields, group membership, own acquired-use receipts, own type/cursor and wallet. It does not consult material heads or a peer's private state to predict a choice. Native execution may subsequently fail against actual world constraints without silently revising the participant's earlier interpretation.

`selection_policy` joins that content into candidates for all 32 canonical cells. Both polarities compete, as do different origins when their input content is available. A 33rd candidate invokes C4's existing contextual qualification adapter, a realization of Integrate accumulation. It requires an owned model from another context, a local owned intention and an actual paid local stock observation. Transfer preserves the old model as a hypothesis and combines its limit with local constraints; it imports neither public authority nor practiced ability.

The declared score combines destination priority, commitment fit, collective accountability, observed/native support and estimated Model A processing work. The support coefficient is 20 after the recorded review amendment; the initial value of 5 failed the explicit new-observation responsiveness test. These numeric priorities are simulation policy choices. Label renaming and candidate presentation order do not change the selected semantic cell. There is no supplied route sequence.

All candidate cells, selected input revisions, reasons, scores, search coverage and input snapshot are committed as audit-only records. They confer no accessible result. Comparison costs `1 + relevant scanned content + foreign model count + 33 + examined bundles`. Work can remain partial, exhaust its wallet or be cancelled. Only fully paid comparison can launch a movement. Native Model A work, Shell admission, material action, communication, acquisition and later consumers are charged separately. Predicted candidate effort is a processing estimate, not a guarantee that the entire future chain fits the remaining wallet.

The default scheduler starts C5 admission with active interruption enabled. A completed admission is not destination completion. An active Shell can preserve the first paid semantic step and cancel the original continuation. The contextual adapter has an explicit canonical mapping to the Integrate accumulation Shell gate; no seventeenth route is introduced.

Automatic material repair additionally requires a matching actor/context acquired-use receipt. The witness earns it by successful practice on another tool, then repairs the held-out target. Merely reading a model, teaching, a successful discussion or a procedure definition cannot pass this gate. This extension does not claim that every abstract cognitive operation is newly trained competence.

The participant scheduler performs one bounded phase per `participate` call: comparison, admission or native movement. Repeated calls continue paid work and then stop. The environment supplies opportunity turns and initial conditions. C7 still needs sustained populations, renewed demands, wider material settings and the complete release ledger.

## Reconstruction and limits

`selection_audit` imports no selector or selection executor. It reconstructs owned/received inputs and acquisition from raw access receipts, independently enumerates bounded candidate joins, recomputes scores, checks the selected maximum, and links the choice to its paid native launch. Shared record/recipe definitions and Model A geometry remain the common specification. Inherited auditors separately check actual semantic handoffs, material consequences, consent and Shell behavior.

At most 128 local items and 128 foreign models are admitted to candidate joins; each cell examines at most 64 bundles, with deferred work reported. Collection still traverses the participant's current bindings. The inactive-history measurement concerns unread exact world records, not a proof of constant work for unbounded owned memory. Shared contracts retain the inherited two-member limit. All 32 cells have automatic witnesses, but two distinct semantic settings for every cell, unrestricted meaning, general cross-owner nesting and a sustained full release remain unestablished.

The controlled choice-withholding arm intentionally violates the ordinary selection contract after paid comparison. Its candidate records and comparison charges remain; it launches no child. Native accounting must pass and the ordinary choice auditor must reject it. These intervention worlds are never counted as valid selections.
