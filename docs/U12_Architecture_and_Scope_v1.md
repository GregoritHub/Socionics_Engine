# U12 Participant institutions and public consequences

Version 1 · 21 September 2026

U12 adds participant-generated proposals, shared practices and maintained public rules to the U11 workshop. It makes the difference between personal correction and collective correction executable. All governance rules, policies, thresholds and prices in this specification are engineering choices. Passing these cases does not validate a psychological theory or establish unrestricted social emergence.

## The common lifecycle

`InstitutionEngine` extends `CollectiveEngine`. Its requests use the existing start, advance, commit and cancel lifecycle, wallets, actor locks, immutable object revisions and exact replay. The U12 operation marker accompanies the U11 coordination marker, allowing the inherited lifecycle and lawful Model A route audit to apply. No material executor or personal correction rule is replaced.

Each institutional attempt costs one unit, the number of received particulars, its declared visit/search allowance and request syntax size, plus the existing Model A route cost. Responding, learning, assenting and recording use the inward U11 path. Other institutional operations use its outward path. Unspent allowance is not refunded. Failed or cancelled work retains its charges. These are modeled costs, not measured processor costs.

Current group and institution versions are checked at commitment. New received information invalidates partial processing. Hidden world changes cannot update participant views. Material runs additionally retain dependencies on the exact institution, participant assent, group and ordinary U11 performance consent. Withdrawal, rule revision or membership change prevents stale work from committing, even when its processing was already paid.

## What is supplied and what is generated

The finite harness supplies people, Model A types, resources, practiced primitive opportunities, a material demand, two prior blame experiences for selected people, environmental observations and an opportunity/disclosure schedule. The example material goal asks for one use followed by a serviceable target with no accumulated wear. The initial group boundary is built through paid U11 creation, invitation and acceptance; it has no institutional rule.

U7 generates the reusable approval attribution from distinct received blame episodes. U8 can later correct its application to a particular target. U12 reads a separately processed, current actor-owned U7 encounter. Its proposal policy chooses approval or self-checking from that encounter. A real threat, unavailable route, partner refusal, genuine outstanding approval requirement, missing information or absent demand is kept separate from an attributed requirement.

U9 bounded search generates the proposed material procedure from the proposer’s received fields and acquired primitive definitions. The proposer does not receive a finished program or a desired vote. The default search spends an allowance of 128 considerations at depth 6 and evaluation fuel 128. Failure retains its paid attempt and coverage diagnostic; this U12 interface does not promise a retained expandable search frontier. U9’s separate search interfaces retain their inherited behavior.

The initial tool requires use then care. The damaged pump requires repair, use and care; it also has a lower wear limit. Both fixtures use the same U4 material laws. This is variation in state and context within one affordance language, not validation of a new physical domain.

## Communication and independent decisions

A proposal is a versioned public speech act with an exact group, electorate, material goal, proposed authority, generated example procedure, reason and readable wording. Delivery and paid reading are separate from creation. Its internal attribution references, sources and operation diagnostics are excluded from the public payload. It does not transmit private memory or give the receiver the sender’s competence.

Each receiver processes the proposal against its own received encounter. A still-active personal approval attribution can produce disagreement with self-checking. A received avoidable delay can make an otherwise willing participant reject continued approval. A received threat produces a legitimate refusal. These responses are independently paid and have their own reasons. There is no caller-supplied acceptance flag.

U12 uses a finite structured institutional vocabulary. It reuses situated disclosure and reading and coexists with U10 language, but does not claim arbitrary natural-language negotiation or a newly learned institutional lexicon. The scheduler offers opportunities; participants do not autonomously invent an unbounded social agenda.

## Trial agreements and maintained rules

An institution requires at least two direct person members. Its exact current electorate must consent unanimously to the same proposal. A participant can withdraw a vote before enactment. Duplicate votes, votes for another proposal, unread responses, changed membership and nonacceptance cannot authorize enactment.

Enactment creates a trial institution, attaches its identity to the group’s Governance facet and records separate participant commitments backed by their own votes. An institution is a governance record referencing the group, not a second copy of its membership. The goal, authority, prototype and wording stay traceable to the public proposal.

An application to the voluntary collective service independently checks current membership, exact assent, actor-owned safety information and material ability. An approval rule requires a single-use steward permission for other members. A blocked application records its actual bearer, target, public rule cause and spent work. The steward can perform directly. These explicit asymmetrical terms are an engineering choice whose effects are visible in the witness.

Successful application generates a fresh U11 constituent program from the requester’s own acquired repertoire and current received materials. It grants no primitive skill. Ordinary paid U11 consent, selection, U4 execution, observation and settlement must still happen. The native world continues to permit independent physical actions under its material laws; a public rule governs the voluntary collective service, not every possible material action or a coercive enforcement system.

At least two distinct completed runs and their processed physical results must support a maintenance proposal. Reusing one run cannot count as repetition. A new unanimous decision makes the rule active. A mere proposal, an imagined success or one quiet interval does not establish a maintained institution.

## Personal and public correction

U8 correction changes personal attribution. It cannot mutate the public institution, its commitments or past consequences. In the main witness Alice’s correction enables a self-check proposal while Bob still negotiates for approval; the attempted public revision fails and the real service constraint remains.

Bob’s later independent correction supports a counterproposal. Fresh responses from both participants permit a public revision. Subsequent work then proceeds through the revised rule. A dispute can cite the actual bearer’s processed consequence. A consented revision that removes its approval barrier can resolve that dispute while retaining the original consequence, costs, attribution history and prior rule versions.

The witnesses establish scoped local correction, not complete U8 re-ownership or general Shell clearance. The original U7 pattern persists. A projection’s public consequence and the later real public obligation are distinguishable: obeying an enacted rule is not classified as a personal Shell merely because the rule had an attributed origin.

## Succession teaching and dissolution

A succession proposal names another current member. Exact collective consent changes the group coordinator and institutional steward together. It does not transfer material ownership or personal skill. The former coordinator may then use ordinary U11 departure. The public rule and other participants’ service can continue.

A newcomer joins through its own U11 acceptance, receives an addressed lesson, reads the current rule, performs a separate learning operation and independently assents. An understanding record is not a primitive acquired-use receipt. The newcomer must practice required primitives before doing the work. Earlier lessons cannot stand in for a current rule.

Participants can withdraw active institutional assent without erasing an unfinished obligation. A unanimous current-member dissolution removes the active public rule and prevents new service; previous physical effects, run duties, votes, consequences and costs remain. Succession and dissolution carry the existing material goal and prototype, so they do not require a newly generated physical procedure.

## Scope and source protection

U12 supports one live institution per direct-person group, exact unanimous consent, two authority forms, finite material goals and existing U4/U9 affordances. U11 still supports acyclic nesting; institutional authority across nested groups remains unassessed. A rule can retain a historical prototype involving a departed founder because future work is generated and performed by current participants with their own skills.

The only inherited executable file edited for U12 is `collective_audit.py`. Its explicit extension permits U12 ratification to revise governance and consented authority without changing group membership or resources. `institution_audit.py` independently validates that extension using raw proposals, ballots, rule versions and chronology. The earlier auditor is not a complete U12 assessor by itself. All frozen R21B members and runtime modules remain protected.

The original acceptance index has three U12 cases. The detailed protocol refines them without replacing their criteria: original U12-01 maps to detailed U12-01 and U12-06; original U12-02 maps to detailed U12-02, U12-03 and U12-06; original U12-03 maps to detailed U12-04, U12-05 and U12-06. Historical acceptance files remain unchanged; the U12 progress ledger carries current results.

The inspector defaults to received actor accounts. Its optional raw audit is read-only and cannot feed conclusions back into the engine. U13 efficiency, U14 release combinations, the separately reserved legacy release seeds, general psychological validity, arbitrary institution grammar, broad political behavior and R21C acceptance remain open.
