# U6: bounded anticipation and continuing autonomy

Version 1.0 · 21 September 2026

## Authority and scope

The U6 section of the supplied post-U5 unified roadmap and cases U6-01/02/03 in the frozen prospective acceptance specification govern this change. The foundation's source-status distinctions remain in force. All new forecast transitions, utility scores, need equations, search bounds, prices, and scheduling priorities are explicit **engineering choices**. No supplied theoretical source establishes them as psychological laws. The existing Model A/Fold mathematics, source cue meanings, and U5 paid content realization are preserved.

This is one continuing finite workshop, not learned general physics, unrestricted search, open social negotiation, or unlimited development. A standing number of useful tool uses supplies demand. Participants must derive plans from their own processed histories. The driver supplies only actor opportunities and permitted observation delivery. Three participants can have different private histories in the same native world.

## Linked execution

1. U5 pays for contextual recall, lawful Model A routing and Crux realization of a plan.
2. U6 evaluates a bounded set of native continuations from that actor's retained condition, visible material particulars, the paid plan, and acquired procedure receipts. A candidate repair procedure appears only after actor-owned successful practice and acquisition. An explicit assistance offer may already support U5's native repair plan.
3. Forecast work costs one quantum per visited binding and simulated transition, at least one quantum. No prediction is published before completion. Candidate records have `hypothetical_continuation` occurrence, an actor-owned Account, exact target, inputs, uncertainties, expected outcome/condition/wear, depth, and cost.
4. The selection operator compares affordable evaluated candidates. A forecast affects choice but cannot execute an action. U4 still pays for the material work, reserves resources, rechecks dependencies, and commits actual effects or a paid failure.
5. The scheduler knows an own work receipt and result ID, but does not inspect the result's truth. It waits for that exact event and operation, then pays to read a delivered observation. Wrong actor, context, target or operation cannot fulfill that wait.
6. A paid appraisal records assessed fields, missing fields, expected and observed values, and actual mismatches. Surprise starts paid inspection. Observation integration uses U5 before the corrected account governs later work. Failure and stopping do not erase work, hypotheses or material history.

## Bounds and utility

The supported horizon is one or two transitions and the node budget is 1–16, default 8. The candidate repertoire is use, inspect, care, offered repair and acquired repair. Two-step continuations evaluate care/repair followed by use. It is a finite declared enumeration, not an exhaustive planning theorem. A truncated search keeps its coverage and unevaluated-candidate count. Recall has a separate visit limit, default 32.

Base utility is use 8, repair 6, care 2 and inspect 1; a serviceable second-step use adds 4, predicted damage subtracts 12, and predicted failure subtracts 20. Pressure can add up to 3 to immediate use; fear penalizes predicted damage; scarcity slightly penalizes consumptive treatment. A candidate must fit its native cost plus two scheduling quanta. This is **local affordability**, not a guarantee that the remaining episode or all subsequent learning fits the budget. Resource exhaustion remains an explicit incomplete outcome.

Unknown wear limits, tool/stock feasibility, unobserved contents and incomplete recall remain explicit uncertainty. Forecasts use the declared workshop model; they do not query hidden feasibility or receive evaluator labels. Changing a hidden material property cannot change a pre-delivery forecast. Received failures can change strategy; U6 does not yet learn a new transition model from prediction errors.

## Continuing participant state

State includes partial native job, suspended work while reading, phase, standing-demand progress, target revision, plan, forecast, selected prediction, awaited event/operation/target, processed observation, failures, fatigue debt, repetition history and exact wait condition. It is versioned in the same object store. Restoration re-executes accepted commands and compares native world, access and command history exactly. No-op wait/stop polls are not accepted commands and do not grow retained history.

The scheduler supports continue, switch, ask, inspect, explore, rest, wait, act and stop. A blocked material reservation waits for a relevant accessible notice before another attempt; an internal lock release is not itself a participant signal. A fresh matching assistance offer can wake a pending help request. The message is a paid request with an explicit recipient-owned observation, which the recipient must read. There is no scripted consent or promised response, and this milestone does not implement a general autonomous responder or negotiation system.

## Needs and world contract

The new `u6.continuing-finite-workshop-v1` definition supplements the unchanged U4 material contract. R21B budgets are not reinterpreted.

| Variable | Cause | Limited effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pressure | Remaining standing uses plus elapsed own paid opportunities / urgency interval, capped at 10 | Up to 3 additional work units and a small immediate-work utility bonus. |
| Fear | Processed threat observation concerning the same target/context, capped at 10 | Damage penalty and a declared stop boundary. Silence does not clear danger. |
| Fatigue | Own charged work since configuration, including separately invoked native work | Fewer units per opportunity; rest can pause unfinished work. |
| Scarcity | Own remaining finite energy/time and processed exhausted-stock evidence, capped at 10 | Fewer work units and a small consumption penalty. |
| Boredom | Repeated observed action/outcome/condition signatures, capped at 10 | One paid inspection of an accessible alternative target. |

Rest costs one energy and one time quantum and removes up to 30 units of fatigue debt. It creates **no energy, time, material, procedure use, or developmental rank**. Food, replenishment, renewable world resources and more general need dynamics are not implemented here. A new versioned world contract is required to add them.

## U5 continuation repairs

Continuing runs exposed two U5 boundary problems. First, a corrected same-revision account previously competed forever with the original belief. Current paid exact actor/property/context accounts now govern that revision; an unresolved correction preserves uncertainty rather than resurrecting old certainty. Newer material revisions still take precedence, and equally current conflicting corrected accounts remain unresolved. No historical binding is deleted.

Second, repeated delivery of the same exact source field previously looked ambiguous because its receipt address differed. Identical source/subject/path/category/value/occurrence fields now share their meaning while retaining separate paid delivery histories. Genuinely conflicting fields remain rejected.

The inherited auditors receive an optional extent callback for U6 and independently apply the corrected-account precedence. Their default U4/U5 behavior and all frozen baseline files are preserved. The U6 raw auditor additionally checks paid hypotheses, action lineage, received prediction errors, requests, rest charges, fatigue causes and observed demand progress. Its checks are bounded evidence, not a proof of every possible execution.

## Deliberate limits

No generalization of competence, Shell recognition, Shell clearance, institutional emergence, natural-language generation, unlimited memory/search, broad compositional closure, efficiency acceptance or release acceptance is claimed. Rest and finite-resource stopping are legitimate outcomes. The acquisition test explicitly supplies practice; the normal continuing workshop awards no procedure mastery. All development failures and incomplete conditions belong in the evidence alongside the final run.
