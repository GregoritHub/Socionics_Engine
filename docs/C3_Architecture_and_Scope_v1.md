# C3: the twelve crossing routes

Engineering contract, version 1. C3 adds 24 bounded route/polarity contracts to the C1/C2 common object, paid work, Model A path, physical law and access runtime. The formal map comes from the inherited specification. The allocation algorithms, processing recipes and authority policy are engineering constructions, not theoretical derivations or psychological findings.

## Content and processing

The finite language contains owned resource intentions, exact received stock observations, conditional allocation models, personally retained interpretations and policies, exchanged contributions, shared expectations, draft rules, exact votes, ratified rules, and decisions. All content has a task referent and context. Models carry uncertainty and assumptions. A resource quantity is an ordinary workshop quantity, not a measure of a personality or developmental level.

Twelve main recipes under two polarities share nine supporting recipes for addressed offers, receiver replies, exact draft votes and downstream use. Supporting recipes have existing formal Crux endpoints; they do not add routes or count as additional completed cells. A main social route consumes its original source and the exact offer/reply generated from it. Its receiver computes an answer at one demand, retains an explicit capacity difference, then uses the shared result at a changed demand.

Every Fold edge incurs the inherited type-specific Model A cost. I–IT uses N/T or T/N composition; WE–ITS uses F/N or N/F composition. There are no single-function shortcuts for the missing row edges. Two-edge recipes first produce scoped candidate work; the final edge consumes that committed candidate. A material command enters the native job only after its paid semantic step, and the separately paid material extent commits an actual event.

## Distinct outcomes

| Route | Accumulation | Expenditure | Later use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Express | Inspect actual stock as preparation for an owned intention | Consume resources according to the intention | Readiness or remaining supply affects a later allocation |
| Share | Clarify personal contribution in an addressed exchange | Offer it for active participation with explicit receiver assent | Receiver makes a proposal or performs bounded participation |
| Theorize | Retain an uncertain conditional allocation hypothesis | Produce an addressed model submission for criticism/use | A changed demand is evaluated using the generated model |
| Embody | Retain the significance of an actual stock observation | Incorporate its limit into an owned decision policy | A later answer or performance uses the observed limit |
| Coordinate | Establish a shared interpretation of observed supply | Establish understood participation under actual constraints | Receiver adjusts a later proposal or performance |
| Organize | Infer a dependency model from observed stock limits | Put an allocator into governed operation under the owner's authority | Prediction or actual execution consumes the model |
| Identify | Interpret shared expectations relative to one's own stance | Accept, qualify or decline them in an owned policy | Later proposal or performance preserves the actor's assent |
| Mobilize | Perform an actual readiness inspection | Perform individually paid consumption from shared intention | Subsequent allocation uses real readiness or remaining stock |
| Institutionalize | Draft a scoped voluntary rule from shared expectation and witnessed practice | Ratify that exact draft through two separate paid votes | Draft assessment or governed performance respects rule status |
| Understand | Relate a generated system to personal limits | Incorporate it into a decision policy | Own answer at a changed demand; policy can guide performance |
| Apply | Consume one unit as a contained material trial | Execute the model at a concrete task demand | Actual remaining supply changes subsequent work |
| Educate | Examine a system with a learner answer and capacity question | Teach it for active participant use through that exchange | Learner uses the acquired constraint at a changed demand |

Accumulation personal interpretations and dependency models support assessment/proposal in the bounded consumer. Expenditure policies and arrangements can support concrete performance. This is a declared consumer policy, not a claim that Accumulation always means private or inactive: preparation and trial routes perform material work under both faces. Shared understanding, assent, rule validity and actual performance remain separate values. Sending a model confers neither agreement nor practiced skill.

## Authority and evidence

Inputs must be current owned bindings or exactly paid-read native messages/observations. Completed C3 records and semantic steps cannot be imported as drafts. Only addressed public payloads can be disclosed. An actual U11 group supplies membership; the present implementation is bounded to exactly two members. Draft rules require a consumption practice witnessed by those participants. Ratification needs separate votes from each member referring to the exact same draft; a dissenting vote retains a declined result. Published rules govern voluntary C3 requests, not every raw workshop action.

Conceptual and consent dependencies propagate through outputs. Changed stances or membership invalidate dependent completion and later performance. Historical material observations remain historical: reading a past quantity does not make it current. Actual material work uses exact current revisions and independently enforces ownership, available stock and paid work. A generated organization cannot confer another actor's custody or authority.

A participant may exercise a particular shared authorization once. Starting an authorized operation consumes that attempt, even if cancelled or physically unsuccessful. C2 and C3 share accounting for inherited C2 commitments, including owned/public aliases. Renewed participation needs a new exchange. Direct inspection is readiness only; it is never reported as consumption or practiced competence.

The independent auditor imports record definitions and the inherited geometry/material validators, but not the crossing executor, transformer or participant selector. It reconstructs paid inputs, typed postconditions, exact intermediate handoffs, outputs, public audiences, observation/event integrity, votes, authority dependencies and subsequent physical uses from raw transactions and the access archive.

## Limits

Route requests, exchange opportunities and initial intentions are supplied fixtures. C3 is not spontaneous selection or institutional emergence. Its public rules are a new scoped voluntary-rule protocol within the same engine; they do not replace U12's richer institutional lifecycle. Succession, general institutional amendment and automatic teaching strategy selection are not added here.

One main request accepts at most eight references. Quantities and demands are bounded to 0–1000 and 1–1000 respectively. This is one consumable-workshop semantic setting. Different quantities, references and actor types do not establish two distinct semantic domains. Mixed circuits, typed adaptation into C2 procedure graphs and nested accountability remain C4 work; route-wide Shell development is C5; automatic selection and transfer are C6; sustained full-release validation is C7.

Performance measurement varies inactive records and independently varies received observation count. It does not establish scaling with arbitrary population, alternate-procedure counts or composition depth. Exact checkpoints still retain/replay history; active access and total storage are measured separately.

## Source-binding transfer supplement

The standard social panel retains a receiver with a tighter personal cap. A separate declared supplement reverses that relationship: the receiver keeps its own cap of five and first selects four units; received source content then constrains its choice to one (Share/Coordinate) or two (Educate). A control preserves the receiver’s prior cap in the completed output, leaving the later choice at four. This tests changed use of received content while retaining a usable output and the exchange, not merely the presence of an output record. It remains within the same semantic setting and changes no runtime contract.
