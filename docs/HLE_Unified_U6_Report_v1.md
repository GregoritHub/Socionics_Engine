## What changes in the workshop

The scheduler supplies its own next work from an actor's standing demand, accessible history, paid U5 plan and available resources. The environment driver grants opportunities and delivers observations; it does not prescribe the action sequence.

In the normal run, Alice plans and repairs the identified saw, waits for its exact result, pays to read and integrate it, and then completes two actual useful uses with intervening observation and integration. Rest interrupts unfinished work when paid processing causes fatigue, and the actor stops when observed work satisfies the declared demand. One repair-stock quantum is consumed. No procedure mastery is awarded.

For a visibly worn serviceable tool, anticipation selects **care**. A matched selection ablation with identical processing charges selects **use**. Execution leaves wear 1 with care and wear 3 with use; the latter damages the tool. Forecasts therefore change an affordable decision with an observable material consequence.

When a supplied serviceable belief is wrong, the initial use fails. The actor does not infer the failure from hidden native state. Only its delivered, paid-read result produces a prediction error. Paid inspection and U5 integration correct the account, after which repair and two useful uses complete. The original false belief, failed action, spending and correction remain in history.

Two observed repair failures caused by the unavailable repair tool change strategy to a paid assistance request. Its recipient receives a separate observation and must read it; consent and a helpful response are not assumed. A separate test supplies a later matching offer and establishes that the waiting participant resumes. In a shared three-participant fixture, different accessible histories produce repair, use and inspection continuations concerning the same saw.
