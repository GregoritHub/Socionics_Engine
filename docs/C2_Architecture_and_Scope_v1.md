# C2: the four paid self-routes

Engineering contract, version 1. This extends C1 in the same object store, access ledger, paid work lifecycle, Model A routing, and material law. The implementation does not change the formal map or claim a theoretical derivation of the algorithms below.

## Processing and content

The endpoint grammar identifies each self-route with zero displacement. The realization uses two paid Fold edges. Contemplate follows N from I to ITS and back; Act follows T from IT to ITS and back; Commune follows F from WE to I and back; Integrate follows N from ITS to I and back. Each edge uses the inherited type-specific Model A path and polarity support seats. An absent path cannot complete work. Alternative attitudes within the same declared family remain supported.

`SelfRouteEngine` inherits `CruxEngine`. The C1 advance mechanism is shared, with two small recipe/namespace hooks. It freezes accessible inputs, pays recall and route work, commits each semantic intermediate, and passes that exact intermediate to the next step. No self-route grants a free answer because its endpoints are equal. Legacy `CognitiveRequest(purpose="integrate")` remains IT → I, Embody. The new Integrate recipes are ITS → ITS.

| Route | Accumulation | Expenditure | Downstream consumer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contemplate | Differentiate owned condition accounts; preserve conflicting supported alternatives as unresolved. | Rehearse owned possibilities; retain a changed attention policy with its hypothetical basis. | A paid decision uses the generated meaning or attention policy; native inspection or use follows. |
| Act | Generate and execute a repair command using an accessible tool and stock. | Generate and execute use of a concrete tool. | The changed material state enables subsequent use or subsequent care. |
| Commune | Clarify reciprocal contributions into a shared cap, with differing positions retained and no commitment permission. | Retain conditional mutual assent as a scoped, one-attempt participation commitment, or retain dissent. | A recipient pays to read the result, then proposes a bounded amount or executes its own bounded consumption. |
| Integrate | Reconcile bounded procedure interfaces, dependencies, conflicts, cycles and external prerequisites. | Couple compatible interfaces under their owner's authority. | A paid consumer predicts reachable outcomes; an organization can dispatch native work using current received conditions. |

The finite content language uses exact scoped typed payloads. Personal contents distinguish a retained claim from a hypothetical possibility. Exchange stances distinguish a capacity cap from assent. Procedure interfaces contain node identity, required facts, produced facts, primitive and authority. Exact recipe definitions and identical payload strings share the inherited compact substrate. Intermediate records refer to their actual predecessors and evidence; they do not embed complete histories. The bounded payload itself is recorded at each semantic stage, so this release does not claim maximal graph compression.

## Reciprocal exchange and consent

Commune operates within an actually formed U11 group. Invitations and membership acceptance are separately paid existing operations. One participant produces an offer from its own retained stance. The addressed recipient pays to read it, then produces a reply from its own stance and the received offer. The initiating participant pays to read the reply. A Commune recipe consumes these generated records; it cannot import a fabricated shared result.

The shared cap is the smaller of the two offered caps. Both original positions remain visible. Accumulation records understanding without granting performance authority. Expenditure grants a participation allowance only when both signed contributions explicitly consent. Dissent remains a legitimate completed interpretation, not a failed participant. A receiver obtains the result only after an allowed disclosure and its own paid reading. A proposed future allocation is an actual transmitted proposal, not a completed material allocation.

Each consenting participant may exercise the scoped cap once. Starting an action consumes that one-attempt permission, including if the action is later cancelled or fails. Renewed work requires renewed exchange. Group revision invalidates outstanding authority. This conservative retry policy is an engineering limit, not a general account of relationships or institutions.

## System integration

The grammar supports up to sixteen interfaces drawn from native repair, use, care and damage. A connection is supported when one interface's produced fact satisfies another interface's required fact. Conflicting definitions of a node and cycles are retained explicitly and block coupling. External prerequisites remain listed; compatibility alone does not establish that they are satisfied.

The prediction consumer computes reachable interface facts from the actor's received condition. Predictions are explicitly model results. The reachable-fact set describes states available along a modeled trajectory; it does not claim that damaged and serviceable are simultaneous material conditions. The expenditure consumer can dispatch the next feasible primitive, then must receive the changed state for subsequent dispatch. Material execution independently enforces resources, custody, exact revisions and work. Coupling does not grant another actor's authority or practiced competence. The witness runs repair followed by use; the accumulation witness predicts that connection while having no permission to execute it as an organization.

## Evidence and limits

`self_audit.py` independently reconstructs content postconditions, paid intermediate handoffs, exact field delivery and binding, addressed exchange, graph structure, and downstream material decisions. It does not import the executor, semantic transformer, or participant selector. The inherited independent material and work audit checks concrete effects and debits. C2 uses the optional audit extension hook; default C1 behavior is preserved.

C2 is a bounded milestone, not the full-release acceptance gate for eight cells. Route choice, exchange opportunities and authored input accounts/interfaces are supplied by fixtures. Automatic selection across the Crux belongs to C6. New route-specific Shell development belongs to C5; complete nested meaning and connected crossing circuits belong to C3–C4. Transfer to a second tool is within the same workshop, not proof of two distinct semantic domains. These facts remain open in the 32-cell ledger.

## Supporting-operation compatibility map

The schema labels `Offer`, `Reply`, and `Consume` name supporting recipes, not additional formal Crux routes. Their formal movements are fixed by the existing endpoint grammar:

| Supporting recipe label | Endpoints | Formal route | C2 use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offer | I → WE | Share | A separately paid contribution sent to one addressed participant. |
| Reply | WE → WE | Commune | A separately paid reply to a received offer; this alone does not complete the main shared-result contract. |
| Consume | I → IT | Express | An actor-owned representation produces a concrete decision record or proposal; material execution remains separately paid. |

These auxiliaries do not close the C3 crossing-route gates or add a seventeenth route. The eight main self-route recipe labels already match their formal names. The 32-cell ledger counts formal routes and polarity, with supporting costs listed separately.
