# Concepts: from theory to executable rules

[Documentation index](../README.md) · [Glossary](Glossary.md) · [Capabilities](Capabilities.md)

## Three different kinds of claim

**Theory/source definitions** say what a concept means in this project. **Implementation** specifies a finite rule, data structure or policy. **Tested results** show what happened under declared inputs and controls. Agreement among these three inside the software does not supply evidence about people.

The [Companion Theory](../../project_sources/HLE_Final_Build_Companion_Theory_v1_0.txt) uses `source_defined`, `derived`, `bridge`, `interpretive`, `probe`, `machine-checked` and `open` tags. `machine-checked` concerns the R21B rebuild or the declared simulation, not the absent sealed pyref or a human study. An engineering choice, such as a generation threshold or integer priority, is not a theorem of Socionics.

## One world, several situated accounts

The object store gives things stable identities and exact revisions. A material tool, a statement about the tool, an imagined result and an actual repair are distinct records. Participants receive selected information through disclosure, delivery and paid reading. They may therefore hold different accounts of the same world. A hidden change can make an attempted physical action fail, but cannot silently teach the participant what changed.

**Fool's Memory** is the project's name for contextual relations, meanings and tensions tied to evidence. Exact particulars retain the details needed to check those relations. Recall is bounded and contextual; neither a memory nor an interpretation is automatically true. The implementation is structured symbolic content, not unrestricted text comprehension. See [particulars.py](../../hle_unified/particulars.py) and [cognition.py](../../hle_unified/cognition.py).

## Model A, positions and processing

In the [sealed Model A kernel](../../baseline/HLE_Rebuild_R21B/hle/model_a.py), a type is a frame mapping eight positions to eight information elements. Position fields include accepting/producing, valued/unvalued, mental/vital and dimensionality. The unified engine uses those fields for explicit processing routes and costs. A type label does not grant knowledge, consent, skill or a successful result.

The current route-step price is the preserved engineering hypothesis `5 − dimensionality`. Both wallet currencies are charged equally under the accepted rules. **Modeled cost** is what a participant spends; **machine cost** is CPU time, memory or storage used to simulate it. They are different measurements. See [cognitive_routes.py](../../hle_unified/cognitive_routes.py).

## The Crux: four perspectives, sixteen routes

The four perspectives classify the form of content or consequence:

| Perspective | Plain meaning here | A realized arrival requires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I** | Personal interpretation or retained response | An actor-owned change available to later processing |
| **IT** | Concrete action or material arrangement | The actual specified effect, not an imagined completion |
| **WE** | Shared meaning or relationship | Scoped exchange and uptake; agreement and dissent remain distinct |
| **ITS** | Systemic model, procedure or organization | Explicit scope, provenance, dependencies and usable constraints |

The formal route is the ordered origin/destination pair. Its name does not prove that the destination was realized. Read rows as origins and columns as destinations:

| From / to | I | IT | WE | ITS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **I** | Contemplate | Express | Share | Theorize |
| **IT** | Embody | Act | Coordinate | Organize |
| **WE** | Identify | Mobilize | Commune | Institutionalize |
| **ITS** | Understand | Apply | Educate | Integrate |

**Accumulation** registers, refines, contains or prepares content. **Expenditure** externalizes, couples, populates or hands it onward. Each route has both polarities, giving **32 cells**. Both require paid work. Accumulation is not a refund or a synonym for private; expenditure is not a synonym for public. A self-route can transform content despite unchanged endpoints. A formal inverse reverses endpoints, not historical effects or costs.

The **Fold** supplies the single-function edges: F connects I–WE; S connects IT–WE; N connects I–ITS; T connects IT–ITS. I–IT and WE–ITS require composition through an intermediate perspective. That intermediate must be produced and consumed, not attached afterward as a label. [Companion Theory §§3 and 9](../../project_sources/HLE_Final_Build_Companion_Theory_v1_0.txt) defines these obligations.

## The three questions behind every result

1. **What changed?** Identify exact before/after content or material revisions.
2. **How?** Show the recipe, inputs, accessed evidence, intermediate handoffs, actors, authority and paid work.
3. **What becomes possible afterward?** Demonstrate a later operation that actually consumes the result, with a matched control.

For example, a generated maintenance plan must change a later answer or action. Merely storing “a plan was made” is insufficient. Similarly, transmitting instructions does not establish understanding; understanding does not establish consent; consent does not establish practiced competence.

## Selection, development and populations

An automatic request identifies an owned outcome demand and accessible inputs. The selector enumerates a bounded set of candidates from those inputs, pays for comparison, and may launch work, continue, defer or stop. The harness supplies worlds and initial needs. The system does not generate unconstrained goals. See [selection records](../../hle_unified/selection_records.py) and [workflow selection records](../../hle_unified/workflow_selection_records.py).

**Development** means a retained, usable change established by particular paid experiences. Local correction, observed response practice, reusable organization and reownership are different operations with different evidence requirements. A **population** schedules actors' paid turns; it does not choose their route for them or invent their capacities. Repeated equal outputs can trigger a stopping rule, but repetition does not establish truth or general mastery.

## Model G and DCNH: what is present

Model G is exposed as a read-only mapping over the same position carrier, with its own names, blocks, signs and grades. Its table correspondence does not assert that Model G and Model A concepts mean the same thing. In particular, G2 Creative corresponds to A8 demonstrative, and G5 Demonstrative to A2 creative.

The [axes module](../../hle_unified/axes.py) exposes four position pairs across value, DCNH formula positions, and integer load/tilt calculations on a supplied field. It does not derive a subtype from an actor's history or feed conditioning into selection. The proposed grade-based energy prices and conditioning ledger belong to unstarted Phase 7. Source transcription qualifications remain attached to the [Model G](../../contracts/sources/Model_G_Table_v1.json) and [axis](../../contracts/sources/Axis_Table_v1.json) tables.
