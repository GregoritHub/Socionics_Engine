Generated formation is approval-only. The other effects are explicit fixtures. Reownership preserves the source material and does not remove danger, consent, resource limits or the need for native competence. C6 and C7 remain open.

What the signs mean here

  • Foreclosure: paid admission prevents initiation.
  • Premature translation: a retained personal response replaces continuation after an actual native semantic step.
  • Forced placement: the replacement remains personal while the requested IT, WE or ITS obligation remains unmet. It never counts as a completed destination.
  • New defensive structure: fresh blame history generates an additional approval requirement.
  • Residual fragmentation: the same source material recurs after correction elsewhere or withdrawal of support.
