## Implementation and authority

Four C3 modules provide immutable request/recipe definitions, pure content transformations, runtime integration and independent raw reconstruction. Twenty-four main recipes share nine supporting recipes for offers, replies, votes and downstream use. Supporting recipes retain existing formal Crux endpoints; they add no new route names and do not independently close cells.

I–IT crossings use paid N/T or T/N composition; WE–ITS crossings use F/N or N/F composition. No missing single-function edge is invented. A two-step recipe records scoped candidate work, and the next step consumes that exact candidate. The material command enters the native job only when its paid semantic threshold is reached. Physical work is charged separately.

Unread or foreign content cannot supply a completed crossing. Only addressed public payloads can be disclosed; generated private steps and output records cannot be imported as authored drafts. Shared meaning requires actual reciprocal exchange. Membership does not overwrite assent. Conceptual and consent dependencies propagate through outputs, so stance or membership changes invalidate affected completion and subsequent use.

The public-rule protocol is explicitly bounded: a two-member group, observed consumption practice, an exact draft, independently paid votes, and voluntary governed requests. It operates inside the unified engine without replacing U12's richer institutional lifecycle. A rule does not rewrite the raw workshop's physical law or prevent every possible direct native action.

Each participant may exercise a given shared authorization once. Starting an attempt consumes it even if cancelled or physically unsuccessful. C2 and C3 share accounting for inherited commitments, including public/owned aliases; switching APIs cannot spend the same allowance twice. Renewed participation requires renewed exchange.

Legacy `CognitiveRequest(purpose="integrate")` remains **Embody**, IT → I. Formal Integrate remains C2's separate ITS → ITS contract.

## Limits and next step

This release establishes 24 bounded crossing contracts in **one consumable-workshop semantic setting**. It does not establish unrestricted meaning, spontaneous route selection, participant-generated opportunities, general institutional emergence or psychological validity. Initial intentions, available resources, exchange opportunities and route requests are fixture inputs. The later consumers are explicit paid operations, not an autonomous policy choosing among the whole Crux.

A request accepts at most eight exact inputs, groups have two members, and demands are bounded to 1–1000 resource units. Different quantities or actor types do not establish two distinct semantic domains. Acquiring a representation or participating in teaching does not grant practiced competence. The rule protocol applies to voluntary governed requests. Richer institutional lifecycle behavior remains inherited U12 functionality and is not silently claimed as new C3 route composition.

**Next: C4 — composition and nested meaning.** Connect generated outputs across the required families, preserve contextual differences and mixed polarities, and make parent completion depend on compatible child evidence without duplicate work or borrowed capacity. C5–C7 then address the remaining Shell, autonomy/transfer and sustained-release obligations.
