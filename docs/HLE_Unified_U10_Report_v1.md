## What now works

A speaker can express a grounded procedure or relation without granting its listener the intended meaning or the ability to perform it. Intended content, public expression, paid receiver interpretation and actual response have separate records and histories. The listener interprets through its own received evidence, acquired primitives and partner/context-specific vocabulary.

Statements, conditions, questions, requests, explanations, intentions and commitments use the same referents and procedural structures. False statements can be contradicted by received evidence. Questions can receive grounded answers. Requests may be deferred or refused; understanding a request creates neither an agreement nor an executed action. An explicit promise becomes fulfilled only after matching, observed performance by its promisor.

## Demonstrated teaching and repair

The main witness begins with a teacher's U9-generated **repair → use → care** routine. The teacher earns a new token, `vek1`, after successful observed use on two distinct tools. When a listener receives a request using that token, it produces a clarification question. The teacher receives that question through its own paid interpretation.

Two actual demonstrations, independently delivered and processed by the listener, stabilize its local meaning. The first demonstration alone remains tentative. The listener then applies the term to an unfamiliar tool through its own U9/U4 paid execution. No teacher capacity is copied and no listener mastery is automatically installed.

A further request combines the learned word with familiar actions: **vek1 → use → care**, with a two-use goal. This complete combination was absent from the teaching examples. The listener composes its meaning, executes **repair → use → care → use → care**, and reaches the observed goal on a new tool.

On a fragile tool, the old meaning fails. Repair and use really happen; care fails after the tool wears out, leaving it damaged. The listener's paid challenge suspends the overbroad lexical use and sends a grounded counterexample. The teacher receives the report, separately tests the distinction, undergoes its own U9 failure, constructs a revised procedure and demonstrates its new branch.

After two successful fragile-tool demonstrations, the listener retains **if maximum wear equals one, repair → use → repair; otherwise, repair → use → care**. A later fragile-tool request and a later ordinary-tool request execute different appropriate branches and both succeed. Earlier meanings, failed runs, spent work and damaged material remain in history.

## Scope and limits

This is a finite typed communication system over the acquired workshop. Controlled wording displays established structures; arbitrary English understanding or fluent generation is not implemented. Material primitives, budgets, observation access and practice opportunities are supplied. The runtime derives procedures, questions, interpretations, lexical states, challenges and conditional repair. The novel compound utterance is a declared test input assembled from grounded elements; autonomous invention of that message is not claimed.

The vocabulary rule requires two distinct successful targets per demonstrated trace group. It can distinguish two groups through an observed maximum-wear, condition or wear feature. Without a separating feature—or with more differing trace groups—it leaves meaning unresolved. A compound utterance does not automatically identify which word caused failure. Lexical meaning remains partner- and context-specific; this does not establish universal semantic consensus or unrestricted conceptual growth.

Communicated procedures become unmastered listener hypotheses. The listener still needs its own acquired primitives, affordable work, physical resources and actual observations. Further U9 retention is available after successful practice, but is not silently granted by these witnesses. Expressing a procedure does not guarantee applicability outside its demonstrated scope.

The harness schedules teaching, testing and delivery opportunities. Broad autonomous curriculum design, population language development, institutions and succession remain open. The transfer cases were withheld from participant practice until their requests, but belong to this declared development panel, not U14 release holdouts. Older U1 and R21B panels outside the inherited 651-test suite remain historical evidence. No performance improvement, psychological validity, arbitrary-composition closure or R21C release acceptance is claimed.
