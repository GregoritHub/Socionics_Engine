Alice’s conceptual account remains unchanged until the consequence is delivered, read and integrated through further paid work. A matched ablation supplies and reads the same successful repair consequence but omits conceptual integration: the later plan chooses **inspection**. Thus the retained account has a tested effect on subsequent action.
