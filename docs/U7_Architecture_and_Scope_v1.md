# U7 — reusable situated deformations

U7 extends the native U6 engine through `ShellEngine`. Its pattern language is an engineering model. It does not establish psychological validity or identify every source-defined Shell sign.

## Stored identities and executable content

A `Pattern` carries an owner, immutable origin, context, source cue, explicit affordance trigger, effects, supporting experiences and origin mode. The actor/context/cue/trigger index finds applicable patterns without selecting on a target label or requiring agency in the target. An `EncounterRequest` names an exact target revision, available carrier and declared consequence bearer. Each completed encounter retains those roles, sources, route, spending and applicable pattern references in a separate journal record. Its native actor-owned interpretation is retained through U3 access and paid binding. Maintenance history is the sequence of such completed encounters and forecast applications, not a silently incremented diagnostic score.

The carrier and bearer are explicit opportunity inputs in this version. U7 does not discover arbitrary new social roles, automatically charge a third party, or create a real obligation because someone attributes one. Actual material events and debits continue through U4; the bearer field identifies the declared beneficiary or affected party for later consequence handling. Public uptake and collective constraints belong to U12.

| Effect | Encounter effect | U6 forecast effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| obligation | Attribute an authorization obligation and wait if unmet. | Exclude affected actions while authorization is absent. |
| salience | Positive bias selects additional attention; negative bias selects reconsideration. | Add a signed bounded utility bias. |
| forecast | Anticipate adverse outcome and inspect. | Change the hypothetical outcome/risk and its utility. |
| exclude_route | Inspect instead of the otherwise available requested route. | Remove matching actions from eligible selection. |
| approval | Add an authorization prerequisite and wait if unmet. | Exclude affected actions while authorization is absent. |

Each effect can restrict itself to a named route. The finite language has a versioned allowlist; adding an operator requires a handler, accounting and evidence checks. Unsupported operators are rejected. The five operators are **not** asserted to be the five formal Shell signs in the paper.

## Paid generation and maintenance

The declared policy begins with no pattern. Two distinct paid encounters with processed blame, an active available demand, and the same cue/context/affordance produce an unresolved accountability material and an approval attribution. Re-reading the same source, a single experience, a hidden message, or activity without demand cannot meet this threshold. The material and pattern are emitted by completed processing; neither is injected in this generated scenario. Originating opportunities and blame are supplied environmental inputs. The threshold, selection policy and resulting approval hypothesis are explicit engineering choices.

Subsequent matching targets invoke the same pattern. Interpretable, processed statements that no approval is required do not automatically remove that attribution: the actor spends work reconstructing its terms and selected intent. The offline assessor requires at least two consequential renewals under adequate opportunity before calling this defensive maintenance. This is a bounded generated-history witness, not spontaneous human personality or learned natural-language semantics.

Separate `PatternSeed` inputs are simulator-only detector/operator fixtures and retain `injected_fixture` origin. The evidence never counts those as generated patterns. A per-actor generation ablation receives the same initial training and resource conditions; its later physical behavior provides the comparison.

## Integration, costs and continuation

An encounter uses existing contextual traversal, the typed actor's current Model A cursor, lawful composed I–IT realization, native energy/time charges, actor reservation, partial progress, cancellation and commitment. Costs are `max(1, evidence fields + recalled bindings) + matching effect count + lawful route cost`. Perspective surfaces retain attributed terms and selected intent along the paid route. Pattern synthesis is included in the paid content realization; it creates no energy or competence.

U6 forecast integration adds one recalled-pattern unit per applied pattern to the existing node and recall bill. Its original forecasts remain hypothetical. Filtering eligibility and modifying salience/risk affects the native action that follows. U7 provides no unsupported material action and cannot make a tool conscious. Matching received inputs are stamped: a relevant paid read or own attribution change invalidates unfinished forecasts or causes replanning before execution; hidden changes alone do neither.

The ordinary U4/U5 entry points remain trusted simulator compatibility APIs. A participant policy uses detached views, owned patterns and the U6 scheduler; directly invoking native effects is not an alternative participant policy in this evaluation.

Whole-engine checkpoints replay commands and verify the world, situated access and canonical history. Native journal and access checkpoints also restore separately. No generalized correction, reownership, displacement, independent mastery, learned institution, efficiency acceptance or release certification is awarded here. U8 addresses retained scoped change and recurrence.

## Assessment and evidence boundaries

The assessor first runs the raw accounting/provenance audit. It then distinguishes no demand, missing/contradictory knowledge or censored recall, threat, unavailable route, actual approval requirements, partner refusal, ordinary disagreement, unfinished/resource-limited work, a single candidate and recurring defensive maintenance. Empty observation coverage is not accuracy. A quiet interval is not clearance.

The auditor reconstructs paid reading, Model A extents, resource debits, material effects, pattern origin, target/carrier/bearer roles, matching evidence, attributed intentions and forecast differences from raw transactions. It does not call the runtime encounter or forecast transformation policy. Exact access replay checks the grants, selected fields and actor binding history. Finite audit coverage does not prove universal closure. Evaluator records retain `Role.ASSESSMENT` and cannot be granted to a participant; changing an offline label leaves participant inputs and decisions unchanged.

The eight categories are material tools, people, compositions representing groups, definitions representing rules, remembered accounts, hypothetical possibilities, addressable procedure/action objects and relations. Their common addressability does not equate their semantics or activate later collective capabilities.
