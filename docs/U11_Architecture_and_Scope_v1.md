# U11 — nesting and collective capacity

Version 1 · 21 September 2026

The supplied handoff already completed U10. This milestone implements the U11 contract in the supplied roadmap and target conception. All mechanism and cost choices below are executable engineering contracts; they do not establish psychological or geometric validity.

## One authority and one paid lifecycle

`CollectiveEngine` extends `LanguageEngine`. The inherited operation store remains the authoritative writer for material, budgets, observations and acquired individual use. Native coordination uses the same start/advance/commit/cancel discipline. Its cost is one unit plus the number of received particulars, a supplied traversal budget, syntax size and the existing Model A route cost. These are modeled quanta, separate from CPU time.

Coordination realizes WE → IT → ITS through Si and Te; inward inspection, observation and retention realize ITS → IT → WE through Te and Si. Individual types retain their source-defined seats, dimensions, routes, polarity supports and prices. A paid binding and receipt retain each successful coordination act. Perspective surfaces refer to the same output objects. No single-function WE–ITS edge is introduced.

## Objects and membership

An executable group has the native `u11.group` identity, a `Composition` facet, an explicit boundary, resources, exact formation/revision dependencies, a coordinator and a context. Its deterministic summary address is derived from that identity and a supported scope; each emitted summary has its own revision and explicit dependency list. A composition can contain another native group, a person, a material object or another finite referent. Ordinary representations of groups remain terminal referents; only explicit group membership edges create executable nesting.

Membership forms a finite directed acyclic graph. Overlapping membership and resource references are allowed. A shared physical identity appears once in an aggregate inventory. Quantities are grouped by actual owner and unit; stock availability additionally distinguishes purpose. Listing a resource confers no ownership or custody. Physical use still obeys U4's exact material laws and reservations.

The creator contributes its own paid participation. Another person joins only after an invitation and that person's separately paid acceptance. A coordinator may link or unlink managed child groups and other referents. A non-coordinator member may leave. Existing duties and physical history are unaffected by membership edits. Repeated invitations cannot create duplicate membership. The current U11 contract requires the same coordinator for linked executable subgroups; coordinator succession belongs to U12.

A finite remembered claim may reference its containing organization. The claim is a leaf in the membership graph, so the reference does not recursively duplicate the organization. This differs from a membership cycle, which is rejected.

## Scoped projections and situated access

Two summaries are supported. `membership` gives distinct terminal member identities and the number of nested groups. It depends on group composition revisions, so an ordinary leaf's description or private state does not invalidate it. Terminal members need not be people. `inventory` gives resource counts, quantities by owner/unit and unconsumed stock by owner/unit/purpose. It depends on all traversed group revisions and the unique material revisions. Resources and members consume the declared graph visit budget.

The internal cache uses dependency identities. Changing a relevant resource invalidates all groups whose inventory used it, including overlapping parents; unrelated groups remain cached. A structural membership summary is unaffected by wear or stock consumption. An emitted historical summary remains an immutable record after cache invalidation. Re-expansion reads retained exact object history. Nothing reconstructs discarded distinctions because no required history is discarded here.

The cache and dependency diagnostics are simulator interfaces. They never update participant views. A paid aggregate inspection produces a scoped record. Explicit authorized disclosure and a separate paid read are still required to receive its public payload. Internal dependency stamps and selections cannot be disclosed as summary facts. A later internal invalidation leaves an actor's received account untouched.

These inventory summaries omit condition, wear, primitive practice and executable permissions. They cannot authorize a repair or use. The assigned worker must resolve and process the exact material distinctions before selecting a primitive action. Physical commitment then rechecks current revisions and real constraints. An unavailable or stale effect fails without undoing charges or previous actions.

## Constituent performance and collective retention

A proposed program contains `work` and `call` constructors. A work leaf names a person, an exact primitive procedure and resource/recipient identities. A call names a previously retained capacity of a group inside the current membership boundary. Expansion is bounded and rejects unsupported procedures, external resources and calls outside the nested boundary.

A run retains the proposed program and a versioned schedule of actual constituent work. Every assignee must accept their assigned indices through a separate paid operation; acceptance checks their own acquired primitives. Each acceptance creates a real commitment with its original run and assignments. Withdrawing permission changes its authorization flag while preserving its unfinished duty. Joining never grants an acquired-use receipt.

Only the next assigned worker can select its step. Selection requires all assignees' active consent, applicable membership, the worker's own acquisition and received material detail. Enactment makes an ordinary paid U4 operation with collective, procedure and consent dependencies. Overlapping runs share the same reservations. A selected step can be attempted once.

The physical result, delivery, paid reading and collective observation remain distinct. Only a matching observed result advances the run and discharges the matching portion of a duty. A failed or cancelled step ends that run honestly. Earlier material effects, consumption, spent effort and observed successes remain. Interrupting and restoring work preserves the run, current selection, reservations and paid position.

The coordinator retains a collective capacity only after the entire run succeeds and it has processed every actual result. The capacity belongs to the group identity. It stores its demonstrated program, performances and context. Future calls still require new actual work, current membership, consent, resources and each worker's own skill. No member receives the group's capacity as a personal acquired procedure.

## Declared limits

The programs and invitations in the development panel are explicit harness inputs; institutions and autonomous collective program invention are not claimed. Procedures are finite sequential workshop schedules bound to concrete participant, resource and procedure identities. The matched detailed program spells out the same work leaves as the nested call; equivalence covers physical results, primitive work, stock accounting and scoped inventory. Different coordination syntax may cost different modeled work.

This is a common graph and work contract, not a proof of equivalence for arbitrary dynamics. Unsupported collective effects require a new explicit world law. Membership cycles, coordinator succession, participant-generated public rules, U13 efficiency and U14 held-out release acceptance remain outside this milestone. No new U14 or legacy release seeds are used.

The only inherited audit change recognizes the U11 route marker in the existing extent-validation hook. The new service overrides membership lineage for its own writes. Earlier entry points keep their historical behavior, and all frozen baseline members remain protected.
