# U8 architecture and scope

U8 adds `DevelopmentEngine` over the U7 engine. It uses the common versioned store, U3 actor-owned access, U4 paid resumable operations, U5 Model A/Crux realization and U6/U7 action selection. The untouched legacy runtime remains the regression authority for its own episodes.

## Distinct operations

| Operation | Executed change | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Release | A paid exact-target binding corrects an unsupported approval/obligation attribution from current processed counterevidence. | Clearance of related targets, broader mastery, or deletion of the original material. |
| Respond | Paid guarded decisions are emitted as actual response outputs for one or more exact targets. | Unsupported physical acts. Tool effects still require U4 execution. |
| Practice | An actual response is delivered, read and integrated into actor-owned retention. | Independent mastery from support, a repeated delivery, an unobserved result or a quiet interval. |
| Reorganize | At least two independently successful retained episodes over distinct targets produce a reusable guarded organization. | Capacity beyond the supported load or beyond the exact context/cue/affordance scope. |
| Reown | Two observed independent later returns using that organization, including unfamiliar targets and a changed partner, alter the original material's treatment. | Universal clearance across arbitrary transformations or a reversal of physical history. |

The response organization checks currently received safety, availability, actual approval requirements and partner willingness before engagement. Its content and maximum simultaneous load are derived from paid experience. A local binding has an exact target-revision scope. A retained organization can transfer to unfamiliar target identities and categories through the same declared affordance. Names, assessor labels and shared definitions provide no competence.

Contextual expansion consists of accumulating separately grounded local bindings and observed performance across targets. Reorganization changes how later targets are processed. The old U7 pattern remains available as historical and residual material; suppression of its application has an explicit retained basis. The original generated material is immutable. Versioned U8 treatment records trace local release, recurrence on another carrier, and later reownership to that same origin.

## Work and access

Each operation has start, paid advance, commit and cancel phases. Recall, evidence, simultaneous targets, applicable examples, Model A routing and content realization contribute to its finite work extent. Failed or cancelled work retains its charges. Both realization directions have paid perspective surfaces. No new energy or material is created.

The runtime reads detached actor particulars and owned retention. It rejects evidence subsets that omit another currently received opportunity source, respects revised received facts and fails stale pending realization. Hidden world changes alone do not alter participant information. Outcome production, delivery, paid reading and practice retention are separate operations. The actor's own response records cannot be fabricated through the declaration interface.

Historical access, native transactions and complete engine commands replay exactly. The raw auditor reconstructs spending, source ownership, local scope, distinct independent practice, learned load, output decisions and material treatment without calling development policy. Full access replay verifies the disclosure selectors that are not present in the raw transaction stream.

## Return assessment

The protocol fixes identity features: original material, owner, context, cue, trigger, response organization and retained load. It enumerates support withdrawal, partner change, renewed simultaneous demand, later opportunity and checkpoint continuation. Actual resource spending and residual attributions are pathway checks, separate from identity preservation.

The offline assessor reports factual agreement with declared opportunity facts, useful executed capacity, pathway support/residuals, and retained access separately. It leaves missing evidence unassessed. A Canon counterexample retains the failure of a two-operation return after a successful single endpoint. No equivalence-respecting proof is supplied, so arbitrary finite-composite closure remains unassessed.
