## 1. What we are building

The engine should follow one content lineage through personal experience, concrete activity, shared life, and systemic organization. A participant can give an experience form, encounter its consequences, share its meaning, participate in changing a collective understanding, construct a model, and bring that model back into practice. Every such transformation should retain enough history to explain what changed and why.

Fool’s Memory holds conceptual relations, tension, and contextual continuity. Exact particulars remain compact evidence records. Model A organizes situated access, available processing paths, and paid work. The Crux names the relationship between the source and destination forms of content. Concrete operations perform the change. These responsibilities belong to the same causal object architecture.

Completion requires all 32 route-and-polarity combinations. It also requires composition and continued use: a result from one movement must become usable input to another without a scenario author inserting the missing meaning.

The initial release will implement an explicit, extensible content language. Full coverage of the Crux within that language is a concrete software target. Unrestricted human meaning, universal Shell diagnosis, and a derivation of developmental altitude are separate research claims; route coverage cannot establish them.

## 2. Source authority and fixed structure

The primary formal authority is **The Crux, Readable Examined Edition, version 6**, particularly §§3–12 and §§21–27. Its sixteen route names and its polarity definitions are retained. Its distinction between formal endpoint equality and realized content history is essential. Version 6 supersedes version 5, including the withdrawal of the claim that the route machinery derives the Spiral Dynamics color sequence.

**The Kindred Fold, version 0.2, §5** supplies the stipulated function-family bridge: F connects I and WE; S connects IT and WE; N connects I and ITS; T connects IT and ITS. I–IT and WE–ITS are not single-function edges under this bridge.

The existing **conception of the unified object engine** supplies the common identity system, arbitrary-target Shell direction, and the separation between exact particulars and conceptual tension. **Generative Semantics of Information Metabolism, Parts I–VI** supplies the requirement to distinguish invariant primitive meaning from positional access.

The content contracts in this specification are **new engineering proposals**. The source gives route names and polarity meanings; it does not provide an executable algorithm for every cell. Naming that distinction lets us implement the model without attributing our choices to a derivation the papers do not contain.

### The fixed route map

Rows are origins; columns are destinations.

| Origin → destination | I | IT | WE | ITS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | Contemplate | Express | Share | Theorize |
| IT | Embody | Act | Coordinate | Organize |
| WE | Identify | Mobilize | Commune | Institutionalize |
| ITS | Understand | Apply | Educate | Integrate |

The following rules constrain every implementation:

1. A formal movement identifies origin, destination, and polarity. Conductivity is an assessed outcome, not an extra bit of movement identity.
2. Every type uses the same map. A native origin influences access; it does not prohibit another origin or predetermine a destination.
3. Accumulation registers, contains, refines, or prepares content. Expenditure externalizes, couples, populates, or hands content over. Both require paid work. Accumulation is not a resource refund.
4. Perspective, polarity, information-element attitude, actual access, and material truth remain separately represented. Accumulation does not always mean private, and Expenditure does not automatically mean public.
5. Formal composition tracks endpoints. Mixed-polarity sequences retain their individual polarities; no rule for collapsing them into a single polarity is assumed.
6. A formal inverse reverses endpoints. It does not reverse material effects, recover spent resources, erase memory, or restore an earlier relationship.
7. A self-route is an identity in the endpoint grammar. Its realized content may change substantially and must still incur the work it performs.
8. A Shell alters a realization. It does not add a seventeenth route or make an uncompleted destination count as completed.

## 4. The common realized movement

A movement operates on an addressable content object or connected set of objects. It records who is doing the work, which holon and context the perspectives describe, which evidence is accessible, and which revisions are being transformed.

Its minimum contract contains:

- The formal movement and a versioned semantic recipe.
- Exact input references, referent, context, owner or authorized participants, and available evidence.
- The demand being served, intended destination, applicable capacities, and stopping conditions.
- An explicit Model A processing path, semantic work, and any independently paid material or communication work.
- Intermediate results, uncertainty, unresolved tensions, dependencies, and residual material.
- Actual outputs, retained changes, resulting observations, and links to later use.
- A terminal status distinguishing success, partial work, refusal, invalidation, and resource failure. Shell assessment remains separately evidenced.

The executor must pass the result of each semantic step to the next. Computing a finished answer independently and attaching a route afterward does not satisfy this contract. Operators may share primitives or be implemented through composition; distinct names do not require sixteen unrelated codebases.

The content language must support at least scoped propositions, relationships, uncertain alternatives, constraints, intentions, procedures, commitments, shared interpretations, and models of dependencies. A claim, hypothesis, offered commitment, accepted commitment, acquired procedure, and actual event remain distinguishable values.

Four destination obligations govern completion:

| Destination | Required evidence of a realized result |
| --- | --- |
| I | An actor-owned change in interpretation, conceptual relationship, intention, or retained response, available to later processing |
| IT | The concrete result specified by the operation: an actual act, artifact, arrangement, or material change; an imagined effect remains explicitly hypothetical |
| WE | A scoped shared interpretation or relationship grounded in actual participant exchange and uptake; agreement, understanding, assent, and dissent remain distinct |
| ITS | An explicit model, dependency structure, procedure organization, or institutional system with scope, provenance, and usable constraints |

A privately imagined group agreement is a representation of an agreement, not an actual shared agreement. A systemic model can be privately owned without becoming globally true. Sending a theory does not make another participant understand or accept it. Acquiring a representation of a procedure does not confer practiced competence.

Completion of an intermediate contained result is separate from a later wider handoff. This distinction lets both polarities have meaningful effects without bypassing access or consent.

### 4.1 The evidence standard for every transformation

**We must be able to show what changed, how it changed, and what becomes possible afterward because it changed.** Each claimed realization must answer these questions from committed evidence:

| Question | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **What changed?** | Exact before/after object revisions and the consequential differences: interpretation, uncertainty, intention, capacity, relationship, authority, model, or material state. Record unchanged content and terminal status honestly. A new identifier, route label, or larger record count alone establishes no development. |
| **How did it change?** | The versioned recipe, actual intermediate content handoffs, accessible evidence, actors, authority, paid work, and resulting effects. Use references to the existing records; do not copy an entire history into each movement. |
| **What becomes possible afterward?** | A downstream consumer that actually uses the changed output in a declared case, with a matched prior-state or semantic-ablation comparison establishing the change’s causal contribution. Distinguish newly enabled work, changed prediction or choice, preserved ability under a tested challenge, and an option ruled out by new evidence. |

Keep **proposed possibility**, **verified applicability**, and **demonstrated downstream use** separate. A plausible explanation of future usefulness cannot close a cell’s downstream-use gate. Comparisons must state what was held constant and any unavoidable differences; when meaningful, preserve route bookkeeping and matched spending while removing the defining content change. Retain failed and unchanged outcomes as well as successes.

An individual event may still be waiting for later use, or may legitimately maintain something already available. Preserve it with that status. Do not report every valid execution as a new capacity. The acceptance witness must establish the particular consequence claimed, including correction or loss of an unsupported option.

For example, in Theorize → Apply → Embody, an observed failed material trial may revise a participant’s model and retained interpretation. On a later changed case, that participant rejects the unsupported procedure or chooses a supported alternative. The trace must connect the trial to the revision and the revision to the later choice; an unchanged-model control tests that connection. This is a proposed witness, not an observed new result.

The inspector should present these three answers as a compact view with links to the underlying evidence. Candidate downstream uses are generated on demand within declared budgets; this requirement does not call for enumerating every possible future composition.

## 5. Contracts for all 32 movements

The following cells define the proposed minimum semantic responsibilities. Their algorithms, grammar boundaries, and examples must be versioned during implementation. “Refine,” “reconcile,” and “learn” require explicit, inspectable rules; they are not instructions to consult hidden truth or to generate a plausible sentence.

| Route | Accumulation: proposed content contract | Expenditure: proposed content contract | Observable consequence required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Contemplate — I → I** | Compare accessible personal meanings and experiences; retain a differentiated interpretation or an explicit unresolved tension. | Elaborate or rehearse an owned possibility; commit a revised personal intention or attention policy while preserving its hypothetical basis. | Subsequent recall, interpretation, or choice uses the changed personal organization. A repeated label is insufficient. |
| **Express — I → IT** | Give an intention concrete preparatory form through an actual artifact, rehearsal, or embodied preparation. | Execute an expression or action that gives the intention an external concrete consequence. | An actual artifact or event exists with traceable intent, work, and material effects. Preparation cannot be counted as completed downstream performance. |
| **Share — I → WE** | Bring a personal contribution into a contained exchange where recipients register and clarify its meaning. | Offer the contribution for active collective use, with receiver-owned uptake and any resulting acceptance or dissent. | Later interaction uses the understood contribution. Transmission alone does not establish shared meaning. |
| **Theorize — I → ITS** | Construct and refine a scoped relational hypothesis from owned experience; retain assumptions, alternatives, and possible tests. | Articulate and submit a model for systemic use or criticism, preserving its derivation and uncertainty. | The model changes a prediction, test, explanation, or admissible procedure. Publication does not certify truth. |
| **Embody — IT → I** | Integrate an observed consequence into personal meaning and contextual memory. | Actively assimilate observed experience into an owned response or stance through the required processing and practice. | Later behavior changes on the supported basis. A received observation or narrated lesson alone does not confer capacity. |
| **Act — IT → IT** | Maintain, calibrate, or prepare concrete capacity through actual operations and resources. | Exercise available concrete capacity to change the material configuration. | World revisions and paid work satisfy the material law; internal simulation cannot substitute for execution. |
| **Coordinate — IT → WE** | Establish a shared reading of observed activity, availability, and constraints through participant exchange. | Use concrete signals and outcomes to establish or revise mutually understood participation in ongoing activity. | Participants adjust their later participation from received evidence; an observer’s private task chart is insufficient. |
| **Organize — IT → ITS** | Infer a scoped dependency structure or reusable procedure from concrete events and their observed limits. | Assemble and put an arrangement of processes or resources into systemic operation under explicit authority. | A dependency model governs a later prediction or execution; regularity alone cannot silently grant authority. |
| **Identify — WE → I** | Register what a received shared meaning, relationship, or membership signifies personally. | Take an explicit personal stance toward shared life, accepting, qualifying, or declining a role in one’s own retained organization. | The actor’s later interpretation or choice changes; membership cannot overwrite personal assent. |
| **Mobilize — WE → IT** | Convert an understood shared intention into actual readiness or preparation by participating actors. | Carry a shared intention into concrete coordinated performance by actors who can and agree to act. | Readiness or execution is individually paid and evidenced. Shared enthusiasm is not a completed physical result. |
| **Commune — WE → WE** | Deepen or revise an existing shared interpretation through reciprocal clarification, including retained unresolved differences. | Enact reciprocal acknowledgment, relationship repair, or renewed shared commitment through interaction. | Shared meanings or relationships change and affect later participation. Mere co-location, message count, or uniform labels do not qualify. |
| **Institutionalize — WE → ITS** | Formulate and assess a system of roles or rules from actually shared practice and expectations. | Ratify and establish a public rule or organization through its declared authority and participant process. | Draft status, public validity, responsibilities, and later enforcement remain distinct and evidenced. |
| **Understand — ITS → I** | Relate an accessible system or theory to personal experience; retain an interpretation with explicit limits. | Work through and incorporate a system’s implications into an owned explanation or decision policy. | The participant answers a changed case or acts using its own interpretation; possession of a model is insufficient. |
| **Apply — ITS → IT** | Instantiate and test a model or procedure in a contained concrete trial, recording actual results. | Execute the model or procedure in a concrete task under current constraints. | Actual outcomes can support or contradict the model. Both faces pay for physical work and preserve failures. |
| **Educate — ITS → WE** | Translate systemic content into a jointly examined explanation and diagnose differences in understanding. | Teach or disseminate a system through learner interaction, questions, and meaning repair. | Receivers use their own acquired understanding in a changed case; attendance, assent, and practical skill remain separate. |
| **Integrate — ITS → ITS** | Reconcile models or systems by making their scopes, dependencies, incompatibilities, and supported connections explicit. | Couple compatible systems into a functioning larger organization with governed interfaces and retained differences. | Cross-system predictions or execution change coherently. Concatenating records or suppressing contradictions is insufficient. |

Each cell needs an example in which its defining responsibility matters, a contrasting case where that responsibility is absent, and a downstream consumer of its output. The two faces may converge on the same endpoint or final action. Their relevant differences must be visible in content preparation, interaction, commitments, or usable outputs, rather than only in their names and prices.

Theorize does not need to solve arbitrary scientific discovery to operate. Commune does not require unanimous agreement. Integrate does not require every system to be compatible. Correct completion can retain explicit uncertainty or scoped disagreement when the contract requires preserving it.

## 6. Routing, composition, and nesting

Model A routing remains a real access and work constraint. The Fold bridge must continue to reject a claimed single-function I–IT or WE–ITS crossing. Composite realizations preserve their intermediate content, actors, costs, and effects.

Self-routes need an explicit realization policy. An empty path with an unchanged quadrant must not grant free content work. A return path may implement a self-route only if its completed content satisfies that self-route’s contract. Any new policy for processing within a quadrant must be identified as an engineering extension, not silently attributed to the Fold.

Formal route compatibility is necessary but not sufficient for composition. The next operator must be able to consume the previous result’s schema, scope, accessible evidence, and actual completion state. Equivalent endpoints do not license replacing one realized trajectory with another.

The build must demonstrate at least these connected families:

- Theorize → Apply → Embody: a model produces a testable consequence that changes personal interpretation.
- Share → Commune → Identify: personal content changes a shared relationship, which changes individual meaning.
- Coordinate → Mobilize: observed concrete conditions support shared participation and subsequent concrete work.
- Institutionalize → Educate: a collectively established system reaches participants through their own learning.
- Organize → Integrate → Apply: grounded procedures become a coherent larger system with concrete consequences.

Each sequence retains mixed polarities where used. A long route cannot be compressed into one endpoint record if doing so loses relevant meaning, access, commitments, spending, or residue.

**Proposed bounded nesting contract:** a parent movement names the child results required to justify its declared output. The parent can claim completion only when those results are present, compatible, and available under the applicable authority. Child failure or withdrawal remains visible. Parent and child spending is counted once per actual operation. Aggregate state must agree with the constituent results in the specified context; a summary cannot grant a member knowledge or competence it has not acquired.

This is an implementation contract to be tested. Crux v6 leaves a general theory of nesting open. Success under this contract must not be reported as a proof of universal cross-level equivalence.

## 7. Shells throughout the Crux

The existing Shell effect operators provide a foundation: attributed approval, obligation, salience, adverse forecast, and action exclusion. The four diagnostic signs in Crux v6 describe a different level of assessment: premature translation, forced placement, new defensive structure, and residual fragmentation. Five effect kinds and four diagnostic signs must not be treated as the same enumeration.

Every applicable movement must retain its intended destination, actual intermediate steps, target, projecting owner, carrier, bearer of consequences, and relevant demand. This permits the following evidence-based checks:

| Concern | Required trace and control |
| --- | --- |
| Premature translation | Record an early substitution or polarity change and the still-unmet original content obligation. Compare with an available supported continuation. |
| Forced placement | Preserve the requested destination and actual result; a result in the wrong perspective cannot satisfy the original contract. |
| New defensive structure | Trace the additional attribution or restriction to the changed demand and available history. Ordinary resource or safety constraints remain separate explanations. |
| Residual fragmentation | Follow the same source material through correction and renewed applicable demand; retain recurrence on the same or another target. |

These are proposed simulation observables, not a universal detector. A diagnosis needs a declared opportunity, sufficient evidence, and appropriate controls. Missing resources, unavailable information, valid refusal, and real danger remain legitimate explanations for noncompletion.

Extend pattern formation beyond generated approval only when the evidence rule for each new effect is implemented. Retain the distinction between generated patterns and injected test fixtures. A deformation must change actual content processing or a subsequent decision; a descriptive annotation is not enough.

Correction must follow the original material and its scope. Release, expanded context and time, unsettled reorganization, practice, and reownership require their own evidence. Successful support does not automatically establish independent capacity. A local correction does not clear other targets. New demand, changed partners, unfamiliar applicable targets, and interrupted continuation must expose any residual.

## 8. Participant choice and efficient representation

Once the operators exist, participants must choose among them from their own current content, demands, uncertainty, resources, relationships, and retained capacities. A candidate generator should expose applicable movements with reasons and costs; the selector should be able to continue, defer, ask for help, or stop. Evaluator labels and hidden world state cannot select a participant’s route.

The four self-routes must be available to this choice process. They cannot be mandatory waiting states, nor can successful development require walking all sixteen cells in a fixed ceremonial order.

Composition can multiply candidate combinations, dependencies, and histories. This is a scaling risk to measure, not evidence that useful information or capacity will necessarily grow at the same rate. Greater route coverage alone establishes neither an information explosion nor open-ended development.

Elegance means using a small set of explicit mechanisms to explain and perform many transformations while retaining the distinctions that affect later consequences. Apply these engineering rules throughout the build:

1. **Share stable structure.** Reuse immutable route definitions, semantic recipes, and exactly equal typed payloads. Keep actor-owned meaning, authority, evidence, uncertainty, and residuals scoped. Equal wording or equal endpoints do not establish equal meanings or histories; storage sharing cannot confer knowledge or competence.
2. **Record changes and causal links.** Retain version differences and references to actual intermediate results. Parent summaries refer to child evidence. Add reconstruction anchors where measured lookup cost warrants them; do not duplicate full trajectories at each level or merge histories with different consequences.
3. **Work from current demands.** Generate applicable candidates incrementally from accessible content and affected dependencies. Bound search and resume unfinished work explicitly. Record deferred opportunities and stopping reasons so reduced computation cannot hide omitted work or starvation.
4. **Reuse only valid computation.** Cache eligible derived calculations against exact input revisions, recipe version, context, actor access, and authority. Invalidate affected results on relevant change. Actual actions, consent, deliveries, and modeled work still occur under their contracts. Cache behavior cannot reveal hidden information to a participant.
5. **Separate active access from historical inspection.** Keep current state and needed indexes efficient; use lossless, addressable history segments for audit. Moving history out of active memory is a future optimization requiring exact restoration and continuation evidence. A summary is not a replacement for authoritative evidence or a new participant observation.
6. **Measure useful consequences alongside cost.** Track attempted and completed movements, demonstrated downstream effects, failures, and deferred work with CPU/wall time, active allocation, stored bytes, and records examined. Report totals and per-attempt/per-result costs. If no downstream effect is demonstrated, cost per demonstrated effect is undefined, not zero. Report modeled actor spending separately from machine resource use.

### 8.1 Efficiency is a gate at every milestone

C1 must establish an instrumented behavioral baseline and a versioned measurement protocol. At each subsequent milestone, retain its measurements and identify the dominant measured cost before choosing an optimization. Keep these workload families distinct:

| Workload | What it tests |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed active task with increasing inactive history, including shared and unique records | Whether active work unnecessarily scans or copies unrelated history; total exact-history storage may still grow. |
| Increasing participants, applicable alternatives, composition depth, and dependencies, varied separately | Where necessary semantic work and representation costs grow; disclose declared search/resource limits and unfinished work. |
| Repeated use followed by relevant revision, changed authority, interruption, and cold restoration | Whether reuse remains valid and retained evidence still supports exact continuation and independent audit. |

Record source/protocol versions, workload denominators, actual samples, cold/warm conditions, and separate active, restore, and audit costs. Set workload budgets and regression tolerances prospectively from the relevant baseline before tuning; amendments and failed candidates remain visible. Compare optimizations with a reference implementing the same semantics and workload. Use U14 for unchanged-contract regression checks, not as proof that additional semantic work has no cost.

A performance gain must preserve the declared outcomes, access boundaries, uncertainties, conflicts, Shell residue, modeled charges, and continuation behavior. Any intentional semantic change needs its own versioned contract and assessment. No performance claim may depend on deleting distinguishing history, silently excluding difficult opportunities, or collapsing unequal realized paths because they have equal endpoints.

Exact unique history has a storage cost. The target is controlled active work and demonstrable reuse with faithful reconstruction; bounded total storage or constant-time execution is not promised.

## 9. Acceptance evidence

Maintain a 32-cell ledger with separate columns for implemented semantics, what changed, how it changed, evidenced downstream consequences, automatic selection, controls, measured costs, and remaining limitations. The three evidence columns follow §4.1; cost records follow §8.1. A cell is complete only when all applicable requirements below pass. A module count or roadmap percentage cannot close a missing cell.

1. **Complete matrix:** every route under each polarity has a validated input contract, actual semantic result, paid work, and a consumer that uses the result.
2. **All type frames:** exercise all 32 movements across the sixteen Model A types. This is 512 movement/type combinations before context variation. Necessary abilities must be established in fixtures; type labels must not manufacture competence. Finite-budget differences may change progress or choice while the structural frame remains fixed.
3. **More than renamed examples:** demonstrate transfer through materially different content settings, including material maintenance, shared resource commitments, and teaching or procedure organization. State which cells are exercised in each setting. All 32 cells must work in at least two distinct applicable settings before release.
4. **Causal responsibility:** remove a movement’s defining semantic step while keeping its route bookkeeping and, where meaningful, matched cost. The relevant later capacity or result must disappear or change. Renaming route labels while preserving contracts must not create or remove capacity.
5. **Compositional use:** the connected families in §6 run from generated intermediate results. Histories, conflicts, and residue survive a return to the original perspective.
6. **Both polarity faces:** compare matched starts where the two faces’ responsibilities differ. Do not demand different final answers when the contracts legitimately converge.
7. **Access and authority:** unread or foreign evidence, unaccepted commitments, missing consent, and a merely imagined agreement cannot produce completed shared knowledge or authorized action.
8. **Partial and failed work:** cancellation and exhaustion preserve spending and already realized consequences. Stale dependencies invalidate the affected work without rewriting history. Checkpoint continuation reproduces the same paid progression and effects.
9. **Shell coverage:** each of the 32 movement cases has an applicable deformation/control comparison, with the chosen effect and opportunity stated. Across the panel, every supported effect and each explicitly operationalized diagnostic sign is exercised. Correction, recurrence, and unsupported clearance claims receive separate controls.
10. **Situated autonomy:** route selection changes appropriately when a participant receives relevant new information or acquires capacity. Hidden changes alone cannot alter its pre-delivery interpretation. At least one automatic witness is required per cell; a scripted sweep remains a separate coverage test.
11. **Nested accountability:** a parent’s claimed result is reconstructed from child evidence, including a child failure, membership change, and withdrawn participation. No duplicate charges or borrowed competence.
12. **Regression and efficiency:** preserve U14 behavior where contracts have not intentionally changed. Declare any changed behavior and its reason prospectively. From C1 onward, apply §8.1 on matched cases, keeping resource accounting, machine costs, demonstrated downstream consequences, and deferred work visible; retain failed evaluations and their amendments.

The raw auditor should reconstruct these claims from committed objects and events without calling the participant’s selector. A separately implemented content validator must check semantic postconditions; the presence of a “Realized Commune” or “Realized Theorize” label is never the acceptance test.

## 11. C1–C4 completion, and the next build

C1 extends the existing native planning and retention circuit with an explicit movement executor over the common object store. Its executed witnesses connect a participant’s scoped model, a real inspection trial, and a changed retained interpretation, with subsequent choice depending on that change. Contrary and matching evidence are both retained. A comparison ablation removes the positive later result while preserving the movement costs.

The work product is the movement schema, a minimal composable content language, a versioned recipe interface, integration with existing paid work and access, and a raw trace that distinguishes semantic processing from route annotation. Include the three-question evidence view and instrumented baseline so causal usefulness and efficiency can be assessed together from the first circuit. This makes the subsequent 32-cell implementation a disciplined extension of one mechanism.

The standard for the project is concrete: **we must be able to show what changed, how it changed, and what becomes possible afterward because it changed.** Find simpler representations and avoid repeated work while preserving that explanation.

C1 supplies a minimal scoped condition language and the common executor. C2 supplies eight bounded self-route contracts; C3 supplies all 24 crossing contracts in a finite consumable-workshop setting. C4 now connects generated outputs across all five required families and makes a bounded parent outcome accountable to exact compatible child results, current authority and distinct-operation spending. Context transfer requires actual local evidence; parent summaries and teaching confer no practiced competence. Failure, withdrawal, mixed polarities and historical material consequences remain visible.

At the C4 handoff, the full expressive language, general cross-owner nesting, route-wide Shell extension, autonomous selection and distinct semantic settings remained open. C5 closes the bounded Shell application gate described below; the other limits remain. **C4 handoff history: C5 — Shell formation and development across the map.** Supply route-aware deformation/control evidence for all 32 cells, distinguish supported effect kinds from operationalized diagnostic signs, and preserve scoped correction, practice, recurrence and reownership evidence. C4 completion is not automatic credit for those gates.
