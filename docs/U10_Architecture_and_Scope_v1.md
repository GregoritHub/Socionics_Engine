# U10 architecture and declared scope

21 September 2026

U10 extends the existing paid native engine through `LanguageEngine`, a subclass of `CompositionEngine`. It preserves the U3 access ledger, U4 material executor, U5 Model A/Fold routes, U6–U8 services and U9 constructive procedures. Existing APIs remain available. A teaching opportunity or receipt is not a new skill.

## Separate records and stages

| Stage | Record / mechanism | Practical meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intended content | Private `u10.intention` | The speaker's expansion of its own acquired procedures and stable local terms. It is not delivered to the listener. |
| Public expression | `u10.message` | A typed envelope and controlled wording. Only its public payload and wording may be disclosed to its designated recipient. |
| Delivery and reading | Existing U3/U4 grants, deliveries and paid read receipts | Delivery alone produces no processed message content. No term or acquired procedure is installed. |
| Interpretation | Paid `u10.interpretation` | Expansion through the listener's own partner/context vocabulary, grounded in its processed referents. Unknown or unstable terms produce a generated clarification message. |
| Teaching | Actual U9 runs, transmitted event references, separate paid receiver observation and learning | The listener reconstructs the demonstrated trace from received actual event fields. Speaker intentions and private capacities are not read. |
| Retained meaning | Versioned `u10.lexeme` | Independent target examples support a local term; incomplete, conflicting or challenged use remains explicit. |
| Response | Paid `u10.response`, local `u10.demand` and `u10.candidate` | A request can create a receiver-owned unmastered hypothesis after checking its own acquired primitives and received initial feasibility. It can also be deferred or refused. |
| Physical performance | Existing U9 runs and U4 selected steps | Actual actions still consume resources, check dependencies, generate events and require observations. A message cannot execute material effects. |
| Commitment | Common-object commitment relation | An explicit actor promise is open until separately paid settlement verifies that actor's matching, successful, observed performance. Understanding or requesting something does not create a promise. |

All language operations use paid actor wallets and lawful type-dependent Model A/Fold routes. Realized language content is retained in perspective surfaces and a contextual interpretation binding. Partial operations expose no terminal interpretation or lexical output; cancellation and invalidated work preserve their charges. Checkpoints replay typed commands to reconstruct world, access and owned language state exactly.

## Meaning and vocabulary

The finite grammar expresses acquired actions, sequential composition, ordered alternatives, conditions and field relations. Owned U9 subprocedure structure can be expressed without delivering its private identity. Statements and questions express relations; explanations attach a stated reason; requests, intentions and commitments refer to the same procedural structures and exact referential slots.

Controlled wording is a pure display function over transmitted structure. It neither parses arbitrary English nor calls a language model. Free wording cannot write memories, actions, obligations or acquired skills. Known expressions can be combined into messages whose complete procedure was absent from teaching examples.

A coined token requires an owned retained U9 capacity and successful observed use on at least two distinct targets. Tokens are generated independently of evaluator labels and object names. An optional spelling can name the same stable distinction without supplying its meaning.

A receiver's entry is scoped to actor, partner and context. Each demonstrated trace group needs positive evidence from at least two distinct targets. Reusing the same events is not independent evidence. Two trace groups can be organized by a received distinguishing `max_wear`, `condition` or `wear` value. The retained conditional must fit every positive example and avoid repeating a challenged failed trace. Conflicting examples without such a separator remain ambiguous. Three or more differing trace groups remain unresolved in this rule. This bounded induction rule is an explicit engineering choice, not a universal theory of language development.

An observed failure of a received single-word procedure can suspend that entry and generate a counterexample message. A compound utterance does not automatically identify which term caused failure; the runtime requires a term to be isolated before assigning lexical blame. Received reports do not supply another actor's new competence. In the main witness the teacher separately tests the reported distinction, experiences its own U9 failure, constructs a revision and demonstrates its successful branch. Teaching and testing opportunities are scheduled by the harness.

Requests produce hypotheses, not retained U9 capacities. A receiver may independently execute and retain a communicated hypothesis through the ordinary U9 successful-practice path. The milestone's main listener witness intentionally stops short of calling that retention step, demonstrating that communication itself does not confer mastery.

## Source changes and audit

New runtime files are `language_records.py`, `language_semantics.py`, `grounded_language.py` and `language_audit.py`. The only inherited runtime-related edits are audit extensions: `operation_audit.py` recognizes U10 paid route extents; `composition_audit.py` accepts an optional dictionary of already checked language hypotheses and demands at their chronological transaction positions. Its default behavior remains unchanged.

The language auditor checks public/private separation, paid output lineage, read evidence, independent lexical examples, the fit of stable meanings, receiver-owned acquisition, actual demonstrations, explicit promises and observed fulfillment. It then invokes the inherited physical/composition auditor to reconstruct material effects, charges and procedure-step decomposition. Exact replay separately checks disclosure selectors and the complete command history. The offline auditor never feeds labels or state into participants.

## Boundaries

This is a finite acquired-workshop language demonstration. Material primitives, Model A source structures, initial work budgets, starting needs, practice opportunities and observation access are supplied. Procedures, lexical output, questions, interpretations, counterexample reports and revised distinctions are runtime outputs. The novel compound utterance is a declared communication input assembled from grounded elements; it is not claimed to have arisen autonomously.

The main transfer targets are withheld from participant practice until their requests. These are development cases, not U14 release holdouts. Exact object references still form the referential substrate; general noun-category acquisition, unrestricted natural language, broad semantic negotiation and autonomous curriculum design are not established. Partner-specific meanings do not imply population-wide consensus.

The learned lexical rule supports sequence traces and two-way observed feature splits. The communication grammar is wider than that induction rule. Existing U9 procedure retention and physical dependencies remain authoritative; expressed procedures are unmastered listener hypotheses, with no guarantee of applicability outside observed scope. U11 collective capacity, U12 institutions, U13 performance and U14 sustained release acceptance remain open. The earlier R21C release is not silently certified.
