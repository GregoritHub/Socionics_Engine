# C5: Shell application to native Crux movements

The extension uses the existing causal object store, participant access, pattern index, Model A progression, paid encounter policy and U8 development operations. It adds one request type, one small executor adapter and a raw validator. Native C2–C4 semantic operators remain unchanged.

`ShellMovementRequest` wraps one canonical movement from the 32-cell map. Exact input objects, target, context, cue, owner, carrier, bearer and demand are retained. Opportunity evidence must match all currently received opportunity sources; a caller cannot conceal a newer received threat by supplying an older subset. Hidden undelivered changes do not change the actor's interpretation.

Two application phases are explicit engineering choices, supplied by the caller:

- `admission`: a paid situated response may prevent the movement from starting. This is prevention of initiation, not evidence of an active-movement translation.
- `after_first_step`: after a paid situated response, an otherwise applicable native movement begins. Its first actual semantic intermediate is retained. An effective attribution then cancels continuation and retains the personal response as the replacement. Any remaining semantic steps, the committed native destination result and downstream use do not happen. Some crossing recipes have only one semantic step; there the interruption is after semantic computation but before retention or material commit. If the attribution is lawfully corrected, the ordinary native movement proceeds instead.

Neither phase chooses which Crux movement a participant needs. C6 remains responsible for situated selection. Direct C4 execution is retained as an explicit compatibility API; callers wanting Shell-aware execution use the wrapper. Native authority, consent, semantic validation and material costs still apply after admission. Admission completion is never movement completion. Cancelled work preserves its charges and first intermediate.

## Pattern formation and development

Only approval patterns have an implemented history-to-pattern generation rule: repeated fresh received blame on otherwise available competent action creates a continuing unresolved material object and an owned attribution. Approval, obligation, salience, adverse forecast and route exclusion can all deform the route-specific policy. The last four are explicit injected fixtures in this release; no new evidence rule is invented for their formation.

U8 local correction is exact-target scoped and currently supports authorization effects only. Scoped correction does not delete the original pattern or material. Practice requires actual response execution, delivered observation and paid retention. Two independent episodes on distinct targets support a bounded response organization. Renewed independent returns, novel targets and changed partner are required for reownership. This is capacity to respond to the guarded opportunity; it does not manufacture competence in an arbitrary native movement. The underlying Crux inputs and abilities must still satisfy their own contracts.

## Diagnostic interpretation

The supplied *Unified Shell Geometry and Developmental Dynamics* distinguishes prevention of initiation from four signs that assume active movement. This release preserves that distinction. The build specification remains formal authority for paid work; older prose calling a ground state free does not override the engine's charge contracts.

The bounded observables are: (1) an actual first semantic intermediate replaced by a personal wait/reconsider/attention response before the original obligation is met; (2) that personal result occupying the continuation while a requested IT, WE or ITS obligation remains explicitly unmet; (3) a generated additional authorization attribution traced to fresh blame history and changed demand, with neutral and legitimate-restriction controls; and (4) recurrence of the same source material after local correction or withdrawal of support. These are declared operationalizations, not universal psychological diagnoses. In particular, attempted personal placement is not a completed movement into the wrong destination.

The engine preserves all supported original evidence. Reownership does not delete a pattern, guarantee safety, authorize another participant, remove resource limits or establish unlimited load capacity. Expanded target/partner history and elapsed paid work are explicit; no independent healing effect is attributed to wall-clock waiting.

## Representation and limitations

The adapter refers to existing objects and steps. It does not duplicate full histories or make a second semantic executor. C5 adds no cache and claims no optimization gain. Checkpoint restoration replays exact commands and validates world/access equality. Exact history still grows. The matching index and recall bounds are inherited; a truncated recall produces uncertainty, not clearance.

The 32-cell panels exercise the finite maintenance/resource language and supplied applicable inputs. They do not close C6's automatic-selection or distinct-setting requirements, nor C7's sustained-release gate. General cross-owner nesting remains open.
