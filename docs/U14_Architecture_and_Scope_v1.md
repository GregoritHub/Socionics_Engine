# U14: sustained evaluation and inspectable release

Version 1.0 · 21 September 2026 · Engineering release specification

U14 freezes the delivered U13 runtime. Its additions are evaluation drivers, an independent per-owner accounting reconstruction, a fail-closed release decision, and a read-only inspector. The 51 native modules and 98 legacy modules retain their supplied bytes. There is no new price, learning rule, psychological assumption or resource regeneration.

## Release boundary

`contracts/U14_Protocol_v1.json` enumerates every case and gate before any release case runs. `U14_Execution_Manifest_v1.json` identifies executable Python files (including inherited fixtures and evaluators) and all contracts. Each release worker verifies that identity before and after execution. `verify_baseline.py` separately checks every frozen R21B member, including non-Python data. Documentation and delivery manifests may be completed after execution; they cannot change the executed contract or runtime.

A reserved seed identifies a prospectively declared combination; the panel is deterministic, not a Monte Carlo probability estimate. All ten U1-reserved unified seeds are used. Development seeds 3101–3105 are separate. Matched mechanism controls deliberately reuse their known constituent conditions across every type and are reported as controls, not novel behavioral discoveries. The panel does not spend any R21C reserved seed.

## Evaluation panels

| Panel | Cases | Scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Autonomy | 160 | 16 types × 10 combined histories; 480 scheduling opportunities; 2–3 standing uses; changed offers, false accounts, delivery latencies, wear, broken tools and finite energy. |
| Matched controls | 16 | One hidden-material pair and one anticipation ablation pair per type, with matched observations and pre-action costs. |
| Population | 20 | Ten combinations × abundant/scarce care supplies; 3–5 individually typed participants; two trial practices and 24 later opportunities; newcomer at opportunity 6, succession/departure at 14. |
| Language | 10 | Teacher demonstrations in both orders; receiver learns a word then executes a new composition with 1–3 additional use/care cycles; partial execution resumes exactly. |
| Nesting | 10 | Child repair capacity called by a parent; subsequent ownership transfer and 1–3 receiver use/care cycles, with exact interrupted continuation. |

The population harness supplies scheduling, invitations, requests, disclosures and initial experiences. The runtime generates attribution, procedure proposals, responses, consents and work consequences. These are tests of participant mechanisms under declared opportunities; they do not establish autonomous discovery of every social opportunity or an open-world society. Material names press/cutter/winch designate workshop instances using existing wear/repair/care laws, not new physical simulators.

Success denominators are separate. Funded positive autonomy cases must satisfy their standing demand. Broken-tool and resource cases may stop or wait, with unfinished work retained. Abundant population cases must complete all 24 later practices. Scarce cases must retain at least one unsuccessful opportunity and complete at least six. A permitted censored episode is not described as completed useful work.

## Independent assessment and continuation

Existing raw chronological auditors reconstruct Model A routes, paid operations, access, generated language, membership, consent, material changes and institutions without runtime choice caches. U14 additionally reconstructs every actor's initial allocation, each work delta, every wallet transition, cumulative spending and final job status directly from transaction attributes. Corrupted debit and rewritten-allocation controls must fail. Audit output and complete raw transactions are saved independently of participant state.

Every autonomy case has three checkpoint boundaries and a concurrently continued restored branch. External delivery queues are retained next to checkpoints; their timing is a world-harness input, not implicit engine state. Population cases interrupt paid institutional work at three opportunities, restore it, and compare completed operations and constituent effects exactly. Language and nesting stop inside actual multi-step execution. Runtime restoration replays retained commands and checks the complete world and access state.

The inherited full institutional witness and segmented-history reconstruction run again. An archive is a historical projection; it is not passed off as a live checkpoint. Failed development runs and release exceptions, if any, remain in the evidence package.

## Performance claim

The complete U13 evidence and its failed candidates are retained. Adoption of those 95 gates requires exact native/legacy runtime, fixture, reference and measurement-contract identities. U14 runs a fresh bounded confirmation: two full institutional contexts, two common workloads at 1,000 inactive records, three native shapes at 1,000 inactive objects, and shared history at 100 and 10,000. All reference/candidate workers run sequentially with other task-owned computation stopped. Active, restore, retained-allocation, peak, archive, evaluator and queue measures retain their distinct meanings. U13's .85 primary ratio and its per-case guards are unchanged. The two large legacy checkpoint measurements are retained historical U13 evidence, not freshly retimed U14 results.

## Inspection

`tools/inspect_u14.py` accepts plain or gzip engine checkpoints. Default JSON/HTML exports show only the selected actor's processed particulars, bindings, own jobs, acquired capacity, pending deliveries and wallet. Hidden material cannot alter that export. `--assess` explicitly adds simulator accounting, chronological writes and an object index; `--object namespace:key --revision N` retrieves exact historical meaning. The inspector verifies that inspection does not mutate its engine. HTML is self-contained, uses inert text rendering and makes no network requests.

## Source authority and limits

The supplied foundation and corrected Canon govern separation of fact, interpretation, occurrence, cost and failed/unassessed outcomes. The U1–U13 architecture/source-authority records retain the inherited theoretical attributions. U14 introduces only engineering choices for panel composition, initial supplies, horizons, reporting and inspection. It makes no new interpretation of a theoretical document.

This release does not certify R21C; supply unrestricted language; establish unlimited scale or development; implement a 3D client; prove general Shell recognition or aggregation closure; or validate psychological/physical interpretations. A passed finite release remains a finite release.

## Versioned resource-control reassessment

The original care-only scarcity gate required at least one unsuccessful later opportunity. Actual release runs showed that this was an invalid assumption in some contexts: the existing generator used additional tool use followed by paid repair, preserving useful work after care supplies ran out. Those original gates remain **failed** in `acceptance_summary_v1.json`; they are not changed to passes. No runtime behavior is changed or suppressed.

`supplement/protocol_v2.json` freezes ten new combinations (seeds 50011–50020) before execution. Targets start serviceable. Each participant receives five care units and one repair unit; independent primitive training consumes one of each. This prevents the unexplored repair supply from making an intended exhaustion control feasible. The original thresholds remain: at least six successful practices, at least one unsuccessful opportunity, exact partial continuation, accounting, consent, teaching and succession. The original 20 population runs remain in the evidence, with their original gates.

The final reassessment requires every original invariant and useful-work criterion; independent raw proof of paid repair after care exhaustion for every failed original expectation; all original regression, language, nesting, isolation and efficiency gates; and every stronger control. The supplemental source and protocol have their own immutable manifest. Original executed source, runtime and protocol remain intact. This is a new engineering assessment under the foundation's requirement to preserve failures and version revised assessments, not a passing result for the original must-fail assumption.

## Legacy regression execution amendment

The original monolithic regression attempt stalled; a rerun with periodic stack diagnostics later terminated with an interpreter segmentation fault. Neither incomplete attempt establishes a regression pass. Both logs and source identities are retained, and the cause remains unresolved.

`U14_Regression_Execution_Amendment_v1.json` prospectively freezes a third execution mode: all 840 identical test IDs in 37 fresh module processes, with the same U13 adapter and no runtime or assertion changes. Every process must exit normally with no failures, errors or skips; all expected test IDs must occur exactly once and all source checks must match. This establishes the complete test population in that mode. It does not establish monolithic interpreter longevity or resolve the earlier crash.
