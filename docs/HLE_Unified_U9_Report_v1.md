## What now works

The participant can construct a procedure that was absent as a supplied complete answer, execute it through paid physical operations, retain it after observed success, reuse it on unfamiliar material and revise it after a counterexample. Structured concepts and procedures have versioned common-object identities, explicit goals and guards, acquired primitive dependencies and preserved histories.

The language supports field relations, sequences, conditions, ordered alternatives and exact retained subprocedure calls. The runtime chooses a condition when the procedure reaches it, using current processed evidence. An immutable continuation remains between actions. Hypothetical search never changes material state; each selected physical step must separately satisfy the existing U4 execution and resource rules.

In the main witness, supplied practice teaches only the individual operations. Search then constructs **repair → use → care**. This meets a standing demand to use a damaged tool and leave it serviceable with no wear. The same retained routine succeeds on an unfamiliar tool with a different wear capacity and fresh resources.

On a tool that wears out after one use, the care step actually fails. Its earlier repair and use remain in history, and the tool remains damaged. The failure generates two demands: deal with the continuing material consequence, and revise the failed generalization. Search constructs **repair → use → repair** from the failed starting case. Independent execution on another tool tests that new branch. Paid retention then produces the condition **maximum wear equals one**, preserving the earlier successful routine through an exact versioned call.

Later execution checks both branches. A further generated composition first uses a serviceable fragile tool, observes the resulting damage, and then enters the revised retained subprocedure. Its condition uses the intermediate observed state. A separate generated **use → return** procedure changes real custody and fulfills an addressable return obligation. Another witness constructs and executes an ordered alternative between two independently learned entry conditions.

## Search can continue and grow

A paid search ticket records its acquired repertoire, evidence snapshot, visited states, partially considered options, queue and deferred prefixes. Existing capacities are considered before primitive extensions. Partial payment produces no candidate; stopping during a subprocedure evaluation preserves both unfinished work and already counted search effort.

One witness exhausts its two-step search scope, retains the deferred frontier, and continues with a larger funded bound. It constructs and physically executes a **seven-step procedure** satisfying a three-use demand. This establishes extension beyond a previous bound in the declared language. It does not establish unlimited feasible search.

The runtime distinguishes a spent ticket, insufficient interpreter work, exhaustion at a declared depth and exhaustion of the recorded repertoire. Running out of energy or time remains a partial paid job. None is an unrestricted claim that no solution exists.

## Limits and development record

These are finite workshop results. Atomic affordances, ticket prices, first-example generalization and the feature-splitting rule are engineering choices. Transfer cases were withheld from participant acquisition and induction until use; they remain part of a declared development panel within the learned workshop context, not U14 release holdouts. Contextual acquisition does not grant another actor or an arbitrary new location automatic competence.

The harness schedules opportunities and delivers observations. The runtime generates procedures, selected actions, discrepancies and the revised distinction. This milestone does not replace every earlier autonomy policy with a general procedure scheduler. The existing U6–U8 APIs remain available; their protected behavior is rerun. General natural language, independently adapting teaching partners, institutions, measured efficiency and sustained release acceptance remain later work.

Cancellation, known resource/permission limits and unexplained execution failure do not automatically become generalization counterexamples. Source interpretation and material truth remain distinct. The raw auditor independently checks accounting, action decomposition, material evidence, observed performance and retention; targeted tests check search enumeration. No proof of arbitrary compositional closure is supplied.

The attached U8 evidence copy could not be opened as a complete ZIP archive. Its name, size and hash are retained in the input-status record. The source archive was readable; all applicable predecessor tests and witnesses were freshly reproduced. Earlier full U1 and R21B panels remain historical evidence where not included in this rerun.

Development failures are preserved in the evidence: scalar disclosure incompatibility, a missing required Concept facet and an overly restrictive test expectation about minimal physical work. Later review corrected conditional timing and interpreter-stop accounting. The final source was held unchanged during the successful combined run.
