## Lifecycle and limits

The actor has at most one active U5 cognitive job. Actual charged hops update the reconstructed cursor, including on cancellation. There are no refunds. Pending jobs freeze their accessible input view; a changed recalled binding or relevant policy revision invalidates their output while retaining work. A changed hidden material state alone cannot change their recalled content or plan. Material feasibility is still checked at native commitment.

Executable plans are rejected after a changed binding in the same context, and material dependencies remain live checks at commitment. This is a conservative rule; broader context-sensitive replanning belongs to U6. Previously completed effects are never undone by an interpretation change.

The native U4 API remains a trusted simulator compatibility interface. Participant policy functions receive detached views, not the engine/world. Replay reconstructs the entire command history and rejects rehashed inconsistent state. Partial jobs, exact historical accounts and historical views survive checkpoints.

The raw audit independently checks Model A adjacency, support seats, fixed dimensionality, work extents, matching budget debits, material accounting, actor-owned paid evidence, retained conclusion values, and surface/action lineage. It does not call the cognitive planner, content-derivation function or engine indexes. It is a bounded witness audit, not the U14 release gate or a proof of complete traversal-policy correctness.

No generalized skill, realistic-personality validation, nonlinear curvature, automatic autonomy, numerical Shell law, diagnosis, population behavior, or performance acceptance is claimed.
