## What now works

Participants can generate concrete institutional proposals from their own received encounters and material goals. U9 search constructs their example procedures from individually acquired primitives. Each person reads the proposal and independently accepts, refuses or negotiates with a traceable reason. Exact current-member consent creates a trial agreement. Repeated observed work and a further collective decision create a maintained public rule.

The public rule governs a voluntary collective service. Applications can proceed into paid constituent work or produce a real, costly delay attributed to the affected person and the enacted rule. Participants can grant one-use permission, withdraw consent, raise a dispute, revise the rule, choose a successor, teach a newcomer and dissolve the arrangement. Newcomer understanding and assent grant no material skill.

## The demonstrated causal circuit

Distinct received blame episodes generate personal U7 approval attributions. Alice’s attribution influences a public proposal. Bob considers it against his own independently retained history and consents. Alice performs two actual use-and-care routines; the observed performances support a maintained approval rule. Bob’s later application is delayed by that rule. Its consequence records Bob as the bearer, preserves the cost and names the public cause.

Alice then completes a local U8 correction. Her changed interpretation supports self-checking, but the public rule remains byte-for-byte unchanged. Bob still negotiates for approval, so the attempted public revision fails. A subsequent application still meets the public approval constraint.

After Bob’s separate correction, he makes a counterproposal. Fresh responses permit a collective revision. His later material work proceeds without steward permission, and the public dispute is resolved while its original consequence remains. This distinguishes personal correction from the actual work required to change an institution.

The same institution then gains a consented successor, survives Alice’s departure, teaches Eve its current terms and receives Eve’s separate assent. Eve’s first application fails for missing personal acquisition; her later work succeeds after actual primitive practice. Finally, the current members dissolve the institution. An accepted but unfinished work duty and all earlier effects remain in history.

## Scope and limits

The harness supplies the initial resource world, acquired primitive opportunities, material goals, prior experiences and the opportunity/disclosure schedule. It supplies no finished institution, material program or desired response. The rule grammar, unanimous enactment rule, two-practice maintenance threshold, authority alternatives, cost formula and policies are explicit engineering choices. The engine generates the concrete arrangement within those choices; unrestricted institutional invention is not established.

This contract supports one live institution per direct-person group, two authority forms and finite workshop goals. U11 nesting remains available, but institutional authority across nested groups is unassessed. Structured public proposals communicate exact terms and reasons; arbitrary natural-language negotiation and a newly learned institutional lexicon are not claimed. The scheduler still offers the phases of the demonstration.

The public constraint applies to the voluntary collective service. It is not a new physical law blocking all independent actions, and coercive enforcement is outside scope. Personal correction in this witness is local to a target; it does not establish general Shell clearance or complete re-ownership. Original patterns, rules, commitments and consequences remain recoverable.

The evidence retains development errors, failed amendments, missing-skill attempts, legitimate refusal and unfinished work. A raw inspector result is included. The final reproduction uses one frozen execution identity; earlier development passes are not substituted for it. U13 efficiency, U14 held-out release, the older R21C release contract and broader psychological validity remain open. No reserved release seeds were used.
