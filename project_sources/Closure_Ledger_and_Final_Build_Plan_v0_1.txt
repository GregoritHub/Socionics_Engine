0. Method and limits
The most logical way to close the open questions is to stop treating them as one list. Each item in the registers already says what would settle it. Sorted by that instrument, the items fall into six classes, and only one class can be closed by work at the bench. This note sorts everything visible to this session, runs the kernel jobs that R21B can settle, and closes what met its own stated condition.
