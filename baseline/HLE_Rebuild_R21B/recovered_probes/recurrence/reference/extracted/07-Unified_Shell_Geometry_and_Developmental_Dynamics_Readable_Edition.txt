P0026 1. Purpose
P0027 This document defines one developmental object and two ways to observe it.
P0028 The Crux and Observational Information Geometry (OIG) are not separate mechanisms. They describe the same realized process at different resolutions.
P0029 The Crux focuses on functional meaning. It tracks perspective, route, polarity, conductivity, Shell formation, holonic closure, and altitude.
P0030 OIG focuses on information geometry. It tracks content state, quotient visibility, persistent change, cancellation, recurrence, and trajectory information.
P0031 In short: The Crux tells us what transformation is occurring. OIG tells us how that transformation is realized.

P0078 I = 00    IT = 01    WE = 10    ITS = 11

P0080 A route is an ordered pair:
P0081 o → d

P0083 Each route has one origin and one destination.

P0088 disp(o → d) = o XOR d

P0090 Crux question: What transformation is the system attempting to complete?

P0131 6. The Unified Shell
P0132 A Shell is a self-maintaining distortion of an otherwise coherent movement.
P0133 A Shell is not simply failure. The system can still reach the correct destination and preserve an acceptable external form.
P0134 The distortion lies in the internal path that the system must maintain to keep producing that form.
P0135 Represent the underlying Shell object as:
P0136 𝒮
P0137 6.1 Crux Projection of the Shell
P0138 The Crux observes five candidate signs of 𝒮:
P0139 Premature translation.
P0140 Forced placement.
P0141 New defensive structure.
P0142 Residual fragmentation.
P0143 Foreclosure / prevented initiation.
P0144 A movement is non-conductive when one or more of these signs persist after increased demand and CAPE.
P0145 Signs 1–4 assume that the movement became active. Sign 5 covers the case in which the required movement never begins.
P0146 Foreclosure: Under a specified demand that requires a non-current movement, the selected movement does not activate. The system preserves its current organization, and the prevention persists through the conductivity test.
P0148 The demand clause is necessary. Without demand, the four self-routes could be misclassified as pathological.
P0149 The ground state is the native self-route of the current holon. It can run indefinitely on two strong valued functions at displacement 00.
P0150 The ground state produces no change of perspective. It is free and non-pathological.
P0151 There is no architectural gradient out of the ground state. Demand is required to initiate movement.
P0152 Rest and foreclosure can therefore look identical to OIG:
P0153 Rest: no demand is present, and the native self-route continues.
P0154 Foreclosure: demand is present, but the required movement does not begin.
P0155 Pure OIG cannot separate these cases because its projection does not contain demand.
P0156 The Shell detector must therefore be joint:
P0157 D_Shell = (D, Π_C, Π_O)
P0158 6.2 OIG Projection of the Shell
P0159 OIG observes the trajectory-side footprint of the same Shell:
P0160 S_O(𝒮) = (K, R, …)
P0161 K identifies changes that became active but were not retained.
P0162 R identifies repeated returns and maintained patterns.
P0163 K and R therefore do not define a second kind of Shell. They describe the trajectory geometry of the same underlying object.
P0164 𝒮 → { S_C: functional/diagnostic appearance; K,R: trajectory appearance; ι: continuous intensity }
P0165 The exact mapping from individual Crux Shell signs to specific K and R patterns remains to be derived.
P0166 7. Shell Maintenance
P0167 The simplest OIG pattern for maintenance is a return to the starting form:
P0168 A → B → A
P0169 The system changed from A to B, so activity occurred. The endpoint then returned to A.
P0170 At the relevant quotient:
P0171 V = 1    N = 0    K = 1
P0172 Meaning: There was observable activity, but no retained transformation at the endpoint.
P0174 A Shell can therefore require internal structural work while preserving the same external shape.
P0175 The important variable is not only how much activity occurs. The relationship between V and N matters.
P0176 A system can show high cumulative activation and low net retention while remaining highly active.
P0177 This is the information-geometric pattern expected from persistent compensation.
P0178 Under observation, distinguish three states:
P0179 Rest: no relevant demand is present, and the native self-route is active.
P0180 Conductive realization: the system meets the demand without persistent distortion.
P0181 Shell-mediated realization: demand is present, but distortion or non-initiation persists. This includes foreclosure.
P0182 Pure A under demand is the observational signature of foreclosure. Pure A without demand is rest.
P0183 These cases are indistinguishable from Π_O alone.

P0238 12. Horizontal Adaptation Versus Development
P0239 When a new demand appears, test the movements that the current holon can use.
P0240 Let C(H,D) be the set of movements that remain conductive under demand D.
P0241 If the set is not empty:
P0242 C(H_k, D) ≠ ∅    → horizontal adaptation
P0243 The current holon can still solve the problem. It may change route, polarity, timing, or strategy without increasing altitude.
P0244 If the set is empty:
P0245 C(H_k, D) = ∅    → developmental boundary
P0246 Now search for the smallest higher organization that can solve the same demand conductively:
P0247 H_(k+1) = min { H' > H_k : C(H', D) ≠ ∅ }
P0248 This is the altitude-selection rule.
P0249 The next altitude is not named in advance. It is generated by the smallest coherent extension that solves the unsolved demand.
