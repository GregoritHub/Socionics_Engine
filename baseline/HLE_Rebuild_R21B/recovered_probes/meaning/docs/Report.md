# Holon-owned contextual cue meanings — report v1

The bounded association-learning experiment passed its six scoped acceptance
gates. Holons acquired different expectations from different experience
histories, attached them to stable card addresses, used them to change recall
and physical action, retained them across a quiet interval, and revised them
after the conditions reversed. No personal meanings were requested from the
user or assigned to Sun, Judgement or World as processing functions.

This demonstrates experience-grounded contextual associations within the
supplied ownership/checking grammar. It does not demonstrate unrestricted
concept invention, a unique advantage of the Identity symbols, or higher
developmental altitude. The fixed always-inspect control was equally accurate
in the settled windows, made no initial or reversal errors, and cost less.

## Mechanism

Each holon initially has genuine retained ownership observations and no learned
scene meanings. Four neutral scene labels are supplied with the demand stream;
their consequences are not supplied to the learner. The first completed
experience in a scene acquires a least-used Identity cue, with a seeded tie
order. Three cues can therefore serve four contexts without moving a card or
forcing a single meaning across contexts.

After an actual R4 action and Embody operation, the learner uses the delivered
first-action receipt to record whether the demanded object was unavailable.
The successful transfer receipt matters: the recipient's ownership after a
transfer must not be mislearned as prior unavailability. For an inspection, the
delivered ownership observation provides the answer. These are the holon's
own consequences, rather than evaluator verdicts or future schedule values.

A tentative meaning contains the scene, at most three recent outcome samples,
exact links to their embodied memories, an encounter count, and an optional
link to a memory carrying a genuinely acquired R4 checking capability. The
majority of the samples determines the expectation that checking is useful.
That learning rule and the available checking procedure are supplied; the
association values and their historical references are learned.

R3 Write retains the proposed meaning and Bind publishes its exact revision.
Until both finish, the old binding remains active. On a later demand, paid
contextual recall selects a published meaning. A paid decision record and
working binding make its selected experience available to the next paid R4
recall. A recalled capability changes the account's guard and makes Apply
inspect before attempting transfer. A context judged reliable uses the original
ownership claim. No checking capability is constructed by the new controller;
all such capabilities originate in actual R4 Embody outputs.

The legal Theorize → Apply → Embody order is retained. The old experimental
Sun → Theorize, Judgement → Apply, World → Embody interpretation is removed
from cue semantics. The same learned cue can be involved in any circuit; its
meaning is not its phase number.

## Declared panel

The declaration hash, fixed before implementation and evaluation, is
`0a1be4d91f8397dc4b2ee05902d6f5cbbd9df50f509871df3052982450bd984a`.

- 80 full-budget runs: four TIMs (IEE, LSE, LSI, EII), two seeds, two opposite
  histories, and five policies; 8,000 participant work units per run.
- 10 scarce runs: both histories, IEE, seed 17, five policies; 600 units per run.
- Two controls: reversed context-discovery order and anonymous display names.
- Every full run has 40 demands across ten rounds, with four scenes and a new
  object on every demand.

Using human-readable round numbers, rounds 1–3 establish experience, ten quiet
ticks precede rounds 4–5, all four contexts reverse at round 6, and rounds 9–10
measure settled adaptation. The raw ledger numbers rounds from zero.

The two histories make opposite contexts reliable while keeping TIM, seed,
initial memories and demand structure matched. External ownership changes use
real R2 transfers. Any participant cost imposed by that external fixture is
credited exactly and audited separately; its answer is never passed into the
learner. Demands, scene labels and the reversal schedule are supplied by the
experiment. Holons do not invent this task or discover an unbounded context
vocabulary.

## Results

There were **92 trajectories, 3,367 completed developmental circuits and 4,113
physical actions**. All 82 ample runs completed; the ten scarce runs retained
partial progress when resources ran out. Total charged participant work was
208,598 energy units and the same number of time units, excluding the separately
accounted initial setup and fixture transfers.

An appropriate demand requires no invalid transfer and requires executing the
transfer when the actor owns the object. Simply doing nothing cannot pass.
Each entry in the following settled-window columns covers 128 demands.

| Policy | New objects after quiet, rounds 4–5 | After adaptation, rounds 9–10 | All 640 demands, including learning and reversal | Invalid transfers, all rounds | Mean paid units per 40-demand run |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Learned associations | **128/128 (100%)** | **128/128 (100%)** | 544/640 (85%) | 96 | 2,566 |
| Frozen training bindings | 128/128 (100%) | 64/128 (50%) | 448/640 (70%) | 192 | 2,591 |
| Mismatched context meanings | 64/128 (50%) | 64/128 (50%) | 432/640 (67.5%) | 208 | 2,651.5 |
| Fixed direct transfer | 64/128 (50%) | 64/128 (50%) | 320/640 (50%) | 320 | 2,417 |
| Fixed always inspect | 128/128 (100%) | 128/128 (100%) | 640/640 (100%) | 0 | 2,071 |

The learned policy improved settled accuracy by 50 percentage points over
mismatched meanings and direct transfer. After reversal it improved by 50
points over frozen training bindings. The same results held in every declared
full-budget learned run, rather than being an aggregate that hid a failing TIM
or seed.

The learned policy was not immediately correct. Each full learned run made two
initial invalid attempts while acquiring experience, then four invalid attempts
after reversal while the formerly reliable contexts became unreliable. Two new
contradictory encounters changed each established majority. Those failures are
included in the 85% whole-run result; the 100% figures apply only to the declared
settled windows.

All controls continue paid retention. The frozen control retains new candidates
in a separate archive while its training bindings stay exact and active.
The mismatched control deliberately consults the paired wrong context's learned
content after training. Fixed policies acquire meanings but do not use them to
choose actions. Consequently these are controls for semantic use, not minimal
implementations of each fixed policy.

Learning cost **495 more units per run**, about 23.9% more than always inspect.
In this engine's current ownership task, inspection costs one
action unit and an empty account reduces R4 content work. The experiment
therefore supports causal learning and adaptation, not an efficiency advantage.

R3 bills recall, decision/meaning retention and binding. R4 bills Model A
routing, content work, action and embodiment. Bounded controller calculations
such as comparing three samples and allocating a cue have no separately
validated cognitive price; R3 write costs must not be presented as that price.
The shared initial setup costs 164 units per actor in addition to the quoted
run budget. The external fixture costs 40 actor units per completed run,
credited and spent exactly outside the participant work totals.

## What the learned meanings look like

For the matched IEE, seed-17 pair, the following expectations were retained
after the first three rounds. “Check first” is a learned local expectation
backed by experience, not a universal definition of the symbol.

| Stable cue | Context | East-reliable history | West-reliable history |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sun | a | Usually proceed from the retained claim | Check first |
| Sun | d | Check first | Usually proceed from the retained claim |
| Judgement | b | Check first | Usually proceed from the retained claim |
| World | c | Usually proceed from the retained claim | Check first |

All **32 matched history/context comparisons** had the same cue and opposing
learned expectations. All 16 full-budget learned runs also retained opposing
expectations under the same card in contexts a and d. Thus the difference
includes outcome history, selected experience and action consequences, beyond
different display labels.

Reversing context discovery changed which address was acquired first while
preserving context-matched expectations and behavior. Replacing displayed card
names with anonymous cue labels left the complete engine journal and episode
records identical. This equivalence is expected: the test does not establish a
special causal contribution from the symbolic names.

## Scarcity and developmental assessment

At 600 units, learned, frozen and mismatched policies each closed eight demands
per run. Direct transfer closed nine; always inspect closed ten and eleven in
the two histories. All ten runs stopped with recorded partial work. They did
not reach reversal, and unfinished demands were not counted as successes.
Across the panel, 377 charged units belonged to unfinished circuits and remain
included in the receipt totals.

Mean full-run learned costs by TIM were IEE 2,927; LSE 2,405; LSI 2,047; EII
2,885. Meanings and settled accuracy agreed under matched histories. These
differences reflect the existing experimental Model A routing/pricing rules,
not empirically calibrated psychological costs.

The 184 final R5 study reports all returned `no_persistent_candidate` for the
Shell detector, `unassessed` for retained capacity, and `unassessed` for closure.
All demand objects were new, so this panel does not establish R5's separate
renewed-study-item capacity criterion. The observed cross-object behavioral
reuse is established by the new experiment's action tests. It must not be
substituted for an R5 retained-capacity or closure verdict. No Shell clearance
or higher developmental altitude was demonstrated.

The shared clock completed 120 funded phases per ample run: 40 inner laps and
13 complete outer laps with three phases remaining. Its 9/3 relationship is
preserved as experimental scheduling. It does not independently transform
Ego/Super-Ego content or prove the unique efficacy of that ratio.

Fidelity tests cover actor isolation, evaluator-access exclusion, hidden-history
invariance before feedback, exact historical bindings, unpaid Write/Bind
activation, energy/time limits, idempotent retry, partial processing and
conditional-action continuation, quiet retention and tamper rejection.

The independent audit caught an empty saved checkpoint for case 0. It was
regenerated deterministically and matched the controller hash recorded during
the original run. The exporter now writes compressed checkpoints atomically and
checks the saved bytes. No history, threshold, budget, acceptance criterion or
experimental outcome changed. The repair record is retained in the evidence.

`evidence/trials.jsonl` contains every trajectory and completed demand;
`aggregates.json`, `summary.json`, `artifact_audit.json`, receipt breakdowns,
test logs and compressed journals support the results. `README.md` gives the
commands to reproduce the experiment and audit. R11 remains the accepted
production baseline; this package completes the scoped holon-meaning follow-up.
