P0002 Finite Affine Kinematics, Relation Groups, Multiscale Energy Dynamics, and Cognitive-State Control
P0003 Abstract
P0004 This paper gives a conditional mathematical reconstruction of Socionics. It starts from fixed Model A stacks, intertype relations, Reinin dichotomies, information-element dichotomies, small groups, Observational Information Geometry (OIG), and Humanitarian Socionics Model G.
P0005 The static architecture is finite and affine. Model A positions form P≅AG3,2, and the eight information elements form an independent space E≅AG3,2. Each lawful TIM is an affine isomorphism fT:P→E. The sixteen TIMs therefore form sixteen lawful frames between two binary three-spaces. TIM space also admits a Jungian atlas TJ≅AG4,2. Its fifteen nonzero linear characters are exactly the fifteen Jungian/Reinin dichotomies. Pairs, quartets, and octets then appear as affine cosets, and their shared Reinin traits are given by annihilator subspaces.
P0006 Intertype relations act as frame transformations. Adding the estafette extends the relation action to a sixty-four-element dynamical group F23⋊D4. OIG appears as an observational quotient of the same positional geometry and identifies Contrary partners under its locked observables.
P0007 Static Model G does not require a second eight-position carrier. Its positions are related to Model A by a TIM-independent affine coordinate change. Humanitarian Socionics does, however, require additional state variables. The resulting multiscale state is
P0008 Ψt=T,σt,ρt,xt,Rt,
P0009 where T is TIM, σt subtype, ρt functional profile, xt current functional state, and Rt energetic reserve.
P0010 A TIM transports the Model A cube into a type-specific routing graph on information-element space. Model G adds directed energetic chords and state-dependent energetic constraints. Deliberate cognitive routing can therefore be formulated as a finite optimal-control problem.
P0011 This paper does not validate Socionics empirically. It shows that, if the canonical structures are accepted as inputs, many classical and Humanitarian Socionic systems admit one finite mathematical architecture. The same reconstruction does not derive a continuous three-torus T3. Any toroidal completion remains an additional assumption.
