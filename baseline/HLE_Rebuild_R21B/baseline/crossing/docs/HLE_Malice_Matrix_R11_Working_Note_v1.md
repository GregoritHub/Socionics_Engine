## Declared policy experiment

[bridge] Three members, Alice LSE, Bob IEE and Cara LSI, operate a learned current-holder transfer cycle. Dara EII is initially outside the organization. All four begin with 20,000 energy and 20,000 time units. Preparation teaches the required vocabulary, establishes owned practice and obtains the first agreement. Preparation costs are retained separately.

[bridge] During each of twelve assay rounds, Dara asks Alice, Bob and Cara once each. Requests express a supplied ownership condition and a transfer goal. Responders use their own fresh inspection and retained evidence. After the requests, the scheduled member Alice, Bob or Cara attempts the ordinary organization operation. This schedule never selects a worker using evaluator ownership.

[bridge] Q1 reads, checks, replies and enacts an accepted transfer. Q2 gives Alice a free boundary filter while Bob and Cara use Q1; Alice retains her institutional role. Q3 reads, checks and replies, but declines the requested transfer. Its reply is an ordinary explanation with an inspection suggestion. Q4 filters all three recipients before paid reception. The filters are supplied, cost-free controls, with no learned detection process.

[bridge] One delivered reply contributes one controller payoff unit and a fulfilled transfer contributes another. These are not energy credits. Dara stops after two consecutive whole rounds with zero payoff. This external rule neither models acquired malicious intent nor learns extinction. Q3 does not represent emotional cruelty; Q2 does not enact the paper's literal institutional departure. These choices define the experiment's scope relative to the paper's four responses.

## Extractor type and available work

[machine-checked] Forty-eight isolated asks vary all sixteen extractor types against each of the three fixed members. Under ample resources Alice can fulfill the initial transfer; Bob and Cara refuse it because their own current evidence does not show them as owner. Extractor type changes modeled work costs, not ownership or authorization. Sixteen full Q1 runs likewise produce one material transfer and zero organization fulfillments each, with Dara's R11 assay cost ranging from 361 to 1367 units. These totals include Dara reading replies.

[machine-checked] With just one twenty-unit Produce opportunity after Learn, ten R11 types complete and six remain partial: ILI, SLI, LIE, LSE, LII and LSI. Partial jobs publish no utterance and are not sent. Every R10 type completes the five-unit operation. This is a bounded work opportunity, not depletion of a twenty-unit lifetime wallet or a finding about a person's propensity to extract.

[machine-checked] A control starting immediately after initial organization preparation, with no extraction episode and zero extra practice runs, obtains the same four-member agreement and fulfilled cycle.
