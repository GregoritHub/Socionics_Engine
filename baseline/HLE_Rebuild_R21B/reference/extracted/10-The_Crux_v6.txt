P0005 The system in plain language.  The model follows content as it moves between four perspectives. Each movement has an origin, a destination, and an inward or outward polarity. A movement is conductive when it completes without persistent distortion. Persistent distortion under increased demand is called a Shell. The model can describe development against the Spiral Dynamics color line, but it does not derive that line.

P0166 7. Fifteen Route Cuts and the Two Sixteens
P0167 Status: Derived for the cuts. Scope note for the relation between routes and TIM columns.
P0168 A route carries four bits: two origin bits and two destination bits. Equivalently, it carries two origin bits and two displacement bits.
P0169 Each nonzero linear function of those four bits divides the sixteen routes into two groups of eight.
P0170 There are 2⁴ − 1 = 15 such linear route cuts.
P0171 This number mirrors the fifteen Reinin-style cuts on the sixteen TIM columns in The Whole Machine.
P0172 The mirror is structural only. The sixteen routes and the sixteen TIM columns are different sets.
P0173 A type fixes an origin through its club, as Section 8 states. A type does not fix a destination.
P0174 Therefore, no canonical one-to-one pairing exists between routes and types. Any such pairing would be a convention and must be labeled as one.
