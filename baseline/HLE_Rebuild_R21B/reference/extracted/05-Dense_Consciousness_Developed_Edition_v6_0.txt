P0087 1. The core idea
P0088 Dense consciousness means strong internal organization. It does not mean physical thickness or constant mental activity.
P0089 A dense psyche connects experience, values, skills, relationships, and long-term direction so that these parts support one another during stress. Lower-level changes can still be intense. The person notices the disturbance and keeps the larger direction of life in view.
P0090 v4 could describe this; it could not say what it was in the model. The engine can now. Under the refined gate A23, a holon under pressure relays to its Super-Ego — the compensatory block — only while the complementary perspective is not retained. Once the tower has retained the complement, the same pressure no longer produces the drop. That retention is density. It is not a mood, a skill, or a chemical level; it is what the system carries back from the crossing and keeps.

P0097 2. Four nested levels, and where the kernel begins
P0098 The model describes four nested levels. Each depends on the levels below it and organizes their activity; influence also moves downward, as a stable value, habit, or relationship guides the response to a chemical change. The kernel begins at level 3. Levels 1 and 2 are below the sealed algebra and enter it, if at all, only as modulation of access parameters.
P0099 The higher levels do not separate from biology. They describe more organized patterns within the same living system. The kernel’s discipline is the converse: the lower levels do not rewrite the higher ones. Chemistry changes access, not informational primitives, not the Model A baseline, not the sixteen-route grammar.

P0563 13.1 Four-layer reading of the podcast scenario
P0564 The scenario describes an acute rattlesnake encounter followed by chronic hypervigilance, fragmented processing, spatial disorientation, stalled learning, and residual fear memories. P0566 Neuronal layer — Function Profile. Persistent startle weeks later is not Function State. The podcast links prolonged cortisol to reduced hippocampal volume and plasticity, dopamine fluctuation to impaired plasticity, low serotonin to impaired neurogenesis. Together these form a Profile shift: baselines move, and less of the retained contribution remains available under load. In 5.2 this cannot be a Role/Vulnerable threshold. If it is anything in the engine, it is persistence of the Super-Ego queue across laps.
P0567 Cognitive layer — CAPE frame. The false “run” signal, flashbacks, fragmented retrieval, and spatial disorientation show incomplete processing under load. They map to four of the five signs: premature translation (the survival charge becomes an immediate story); forced placement (the charge goes to a mismatched destination); residual fragmentation (the material still produces signs after CAPE 1–3); and foreclosure (“stalled learning” and the “continuous state of survival”) — under a demand that requires a non-current movement, the movement does not initiate. Spatial disruption can also impair Si simultaneous-state indexing or Ni temporal indexing. Applying CAPE: Release holds the raw charge without an immediate story; Leadership expands Situation and Time and re-anchors the map; Reversion tolerates the trough; Re-ownership re-applies real demand. Conductivity returns only when no sign remains.
P0568 Integrated psyche — density and altitude. A “continuous state of survival” is an arrested generator: C(H_k, D) = ∅ with no minimal higher closure found, because the load prevents the crossing from completing. Density cannot contain the chemical load; the relay drops under pressure because the complement is not retained; foreclosure holds the seat. Repeated successful CAPE cycles restore availability; each closure adds to retention. The weather then stays within the climate of direction.
