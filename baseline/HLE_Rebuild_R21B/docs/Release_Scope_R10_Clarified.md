# R10 release scope

This release is a standard-library Python simulation, with deterministic command
execution, retained evidence and replay. It is the bounded engine specified by
the ten-milestone rebuild roadmap. The original theory documents and historical
implementation archive remain included in `reference/`.

## Implemented mechanisms

The same journal connects explicit world facts and observations, relational
Fool's Memory, paid Crux transformations and Model A processing, IDEA assessment,
independently adapting Socion exchanges, contextual recursive composition,
acquired symbolic language, and participant-selected recurring organizations.
Actor access is separate from the simulator's Truth reference. Incorrect beliefs,
costly failures, incomplete jobs and historical versions remain inspectable.

The participant-generated organization family supplies current-holder
responsibility, inspection prefixes, one final ownership transfer, and cyclic
allocation among exact consenting members. Concrete procedures come from owned
successful experience; revision, received departures, newcomer acquisition and
fresh agreement have consequential behavior. R10 evaluates this existing family;
it does not silently replace its supplied scheduler with an autonomy claim.

## What the release does not establish

| Claim | Current boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Open-ended game or living world | No rendered game, terrain, survival ecology, romance or births. Configured actors and one ownership domain are the sustained test environment. |
| Autonomous motivation | R6 has its declared adaptive exchange policy; R9/R10 organizational lifecycle opportunities and scheduling are supplied externally. |
| Arbitrary language or institutions | Acquired finite symbolic meanings and finite executable procedures; no English negotiation or unrestricted rule invention. |
| Universal information metabolism theory | Operational rules retain source grades. Passing simulation tests is not empirical psychological calibration. |
| Generated Shells and clearance | Existing bounded assessment and correction cases remain; sustained spontaneous generation/clearance is not established by the R10 institution panel. |
| Recursive mathematical closure | Contextual DAG composition is a tested proposal. General recursive Cross coherence and the Canon's all-finite-composition condition remain unproved. |
| Id/Identity cycle | No new cycle has been elected or proved. |
| Bounded memory or arbitrary scaling | Journal, receipts, jobs, indexes and checkpoints grow. The R6 controller's unscheduled notification queue also accumulates. Full all-member consent incurs quadratic communication in cohort size. R10 reports a finite measured operating range. |
| Live-session migration | Earlier loaders remain, including the unchanged `hle-r9-v1` organization checkpoint schema. Automatic translation from earlier live schemas is not supplied. |

Resource credits in replenished experiments are explicit external interventions.
Those runs establish resumability and accounting under the chosen inputs, not
self-sustaining energy production. Workload seeds change profiles and opportunity
ordering; Python hash seeds separately check deterministic container ordering.

The source, acceptance plan, complete command journals, per-case results and
release checks permit the stated conclusions to be reproduced and challenged.
Further product or theoretical goals require their own scope and evidence.

**Active-element limitation — post-release clarification, 17 September 2026.** In the shipped R8–R10 organizational workloads, every profile starts at Ne and R8 language and R9 organization operations use their own declared semantic/procedural costs without invoking R4 Model A routing or changing the active element. Messages therefore emit Ne at completed-operation boundaries; the observed type effects enter through the inherited R6 reception route, price and resource-limited progress for that constant element, rather than type-dependent routing of language or organizational deliberation. Reception costs in the supplied default probe range from 2 to 12 units according to the receiver’s Ne seat. Partially funded reception can temporarily activate other elements before returning to Ne, so this is a Ne-to-Ne reception regime, not a literal every-tick absorbing state. The sustained results do not establish general type-dependent organizational information metabolism or classify types as intrinsically cheap or costly. Changing the initial element, emission convention or semantic routing would require a new explicit modeling declaration and evaluation; the original R10 release and evidence remain unchanged.
