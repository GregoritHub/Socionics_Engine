## What changed

`SocionWorld` extends R5's `AssessedWorld` and single journal. Each participant has its own observation suffix, memory and cue history, acquired checking capability, active Model A state, resources, queued goals and response policy. A pure local policy selects the next operation from those inputs. It cannot read Truth, another participant's private memory, or an assessment verdict.

Participants answer from retained experience, inspect before replying when their acquired lesson or configured policy calls for it, remain silent, wait for testimony, or proceed using their own account. These are conditional choices in a supplied finite grammar. Answers and checking lessons are not assigned by a reciprocal script.

Messages participate in a paid source-to-receiver processing chain. The emitted information element maps through the inherited intertype frame relation to its receiver seat. Paid reception changes the receiver's active element; R3 retention and recall then feed R4 Theorize, Apply and Embody. Incomplete reception, memory work and Crux processing retain their original specifications and paid progress.

The integrated demonstration has **105 journal transactions**. Alice transfers the box to Bob; Bob returns it. Each leg includes a locally generated query/reply, paid reception, retained testimony, recall into a checkable account, action and retained consequences. The declared dyadic identity return, path budget and consequential interpretation tests are **established**. WE initiation, definition and engagement are established for this declaration; alignment over arbitrary compositions remains **unassessed**.

The demonstration spends 85 energy/time quanta for Alice and 114 for Bob, including initial memory preparation. These are simulated work units under the stated price policies, not measurements of human metabolism.

In a separate history fixture, both participants encounter a discrepancy between their own retained account and a direct action consequence. Each independently acquires its own checking capability through the unchanged R4 Embody operation. Hidden world interventions and goals are supplied; lessons, answers and Shell outcomes are not injected.

The retained-history control restores the **same checkpoint** in both branches. Alice starts each branch with 4,906 energy and time quanta; Bob starts with 4,871. A prospective control changes only access to the already acquired lessons. Alice then faces the tool, a different object from the box on which she acquired her guard:

The lesson changes both the source's behavior and the receiver's processing under matched prior history and funds. A further four-condition control varies source and receiver lesson access independently: source access alone changes reply preparation; receiver access alone changes its first action. A separate test checks reuse in both directions on each participant's other object.

Delivery alone cannot establish this result: the tests require reception, retention, recalled content and application.

These controls distinguish type-dependent processing from unequal information, acquired history and configured response behavior. They do not establish a universal compatibility ranking or empirically calibrated Socionics dynamics.
