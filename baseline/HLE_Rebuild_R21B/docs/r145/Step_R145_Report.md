# R14.5 integration correction report

**The architectural correction is implemented and passes its bounded workshop checks. Particulars now use direct paid retrieval; conceptual relations, unresolved tension and retained practice occupy a separate personal structure. R15 remains next.**

This is a working integration of the proposed division of responsibility. It is not a complete executable semantics for every card, a generated Shell, full individuation or clearance. The parent R12–R21 extension remains 3/10 complete, with seven milestones remaining; R14.5 is an additional completed prerequisite.

## What changed

The new `ConceptualWorld` extends R14 in the same causal journal. Detail writes no longer create card bindings. Exact-key queries preserve owner isolation, temporal filtering, claim status and historical provenance, and cannot substitute for executable capacity access.

Shared definitions are immutable and version-sealed. Personal conceptual states contain learned relations, unresolved constraints, practice status, a stable conceptual address, new evidence references and a previous-state link. The old unresolved states survive when their tension resolves. No copy of every date, name or tool fact is embedded in the conceptual graph.

The Fold supplies explicit family edges; Model A retains its three-coordinate positions and all type distinctions while determining actual routing and cost. Every conceptual job records its formal Crux movements and paid route. A composed row crossing is never treated as a primitive edge or a proof of acquired individuation.

The default demo now uses this runtime. The original R14 class and R13 symbolic APIs remain available for comparison and compatibility. Old checkpoint import preserves the historical journal before the corrected architecture starts doing new work.

## Measured costs

One deterministic standard workshop run, with the same base domain:

| Configuration | Journal events | Logical work units | Detail bindings | Checkpoint bytes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| R14 | 188 | 1,839 | 11 | 2,131,811 |
| Direct details only | 131 | 1,521 | 0 | 1,503,605 |
| Integrated R14.5 | 150 | 2,126 | 0 | 1,676,123 |

Direct detail retention reduces logical work by about 17% and checkpoint bytes by about 29% in this case. The integrated version still has a checkpoint about 21% smaller than R14, while logical work increases by about 16% because conceptual integration and exploration are now paid. The details-only comparison isolates the memory change; it is not the full developmental candidate.

A requested single-record detail lookup costs two logical units at 0, 100 and 1,000 additional inactive ticks. An instrumented journal rejects iteration during that lookup. Full checkpoint bytes and serialization time still grow with history. Three wall-time samples per configuration are recorded, but they are short, environment-dependent runs and do not establish a broad speed advantage or R21 efficiency acceptance.

## Source and verification changes

The source-defined suit readings, card identities and conditional Fold edges are preserved. The supplied documents do not define the complete executable meaning of every rank. The Hierophant/accepted-agreement reading and the care/release learning and inspection policy are explicitly marked experimental. No missing canonical meaning was invented and presented as supplied fact.

Two inherited source-byte assertions were amended because the old memory files intentionally changed. Their originals, initial failures, preserved old source and declared new hashes are included. Behavioral assertions and frozen developmental acceptance criteria were not weakened. Implementation failures and repairs are documented in `Test_Evolution_R145.md`.

## What remains open

This release covers one finite conceptual grammar. It does not generate arbitrary concepts, revise every possible distorted realization, supply full semantic meanings for all ranks, or establish a special advantage over a generic relational learner. The supplied exploration rule makes the history contrast causal in this model; it does not establish a universal law of human cognition.

Live supply is scoped guidance available at decision time, not a complete theory of continuous interpersonal scaffolding. The learner still must do the actual care and return. New conceptual state is reconstructed from history on migration, rather than silently credited as previously paid conceptual processing.

Exact historical detail revisions and journals remain large. Current catalogue selection and full checkpoint/replay costs remain scaling concerns. The fixed conceptual address used here does not demonstrate arbitrary file/depth navigation or a geometric equivalence of the 4D, 3D, 2D and 1D structures.

**Next: R15 — generated compensation and material ownership.** Build on the conceptual organization now available, and require a self-maintaining deformation with recurring work and traceable material/carrier consequences. The present uncertainty and ordinary learning must remain negative controls, not be relabeled as Shells.
