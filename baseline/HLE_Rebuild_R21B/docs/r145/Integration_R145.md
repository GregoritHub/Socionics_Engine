# R14.5 particulars and conceptual integration

R14.5 inserts an architectural correction before R15. The runnable entry point is `hle.conceptual.ConceptualWorld`; `python -m hle` uses it. R14 remains available as an explicit comparison implementation. The original R12–R21 progress remains 3/10, with this inserted prerequisite completed separately.

## Division of responsibility

| Component | Responsibility | Implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Particulars | Exact owned observations, assertions, referents, times, provenance, procedures and historical revisions | Existing immutable journal and `MemoryRevision`, with new paid `DetailQuery` by exact local keys |
| Shared conceptual references | Stable relations, supplied suit readings, card identities, source meanings and Fold edges | Immutable `concept_structure` tables; reference seal checked at restore |
| Personal conceptual realization | Acquired relations, unresolved constraints, practice status and exact evidence links | `ConceptState`, stored separately from the particular-memory heads |
| Fool's Memory address | Folded position, file, depth and supplied contextual apex | `ConceptAddress`; rank, folded position and unresolved material remain distinct |
| Model A | Owner-specific seats, three-coordinate positions, internal routing, work and active-element continuation | Existing mathematical core and paid routes chosen through the Fold bridge |
| Crux | Explicit perspective movements | Four recorded edges in the bounded experimental care/release circuit |
| Actual operations | Inspect, lend, use, clean, return, teach, supply and integrate | Existing workshop plus typed conceptual commands in one journal |

Detail lookup does not require a card, binding or scene reconstruction. It is still paid, owner-scoped and temporally filtered. It neither follows conceptual links nor executes a retained rule. Current procedures and R13 learned meanings retain their stricter contextual-access requirements. Old symbolic APIs remain for compatibility; new workshop detail retention no longer creates symbolic bindings.

Personal conceptual states contain relation triples and evidence references, not copied dates, names, tool facts or episode bodies. A state stores only its new evidence and prior-state reference. Historical unresolved material remains reconstructible after resolution. This is an indexed separation with fewer bindings, not a claim of optimal compression or bounded total storage.

## Source status and the semantic limit

The current user clarification takes precedence over the earlier manual's general factual-recall wording. The supplied manual fixes eight suit readings and card identities, ranks and folds. It supplies examples for the four minor tens and three Identity Arcana. It does **not** supply executable semantic definitions for every rank. Undefined rank meanings are therefore not filled with invented canon.

The shared registry keeps all 130 card identities and eight supplied suit readings. The workshop concept is an explicitly declared implementation hypothesis: an accepted loan relates use, care and release under its agreed conditions. Its association with Coin and the Hierophant is a proposed domain reading. It does not define the complete meaning of the Hierophant, turn all help into debt, or prove eternal meaning empirically.

The four-coordinate conceptual address preserves the user's architectural convention. This small implementation exercises one file/depth/context configuration. It does not prove a common geometric projection from Fool's Memory through Model A to the Crux, or implement unrestricted concept generation.

The Kindred Fold's conditional family bridge is implemented as F: I–WE, T: IT–ITS, N: I–ITS, S: IT–WE. All eight elements retain their distinct Model A seats and all four type coordinates. I–IT and WE–ITS remain absent as primitive family edges. A composed formal route is not evidence of individuation. This workshop's I–WE–IT–WE–I sequence is an engineering choice, not a compulsory universal developmental order.

## Learning and consequential selection

An actor's permitted loan observations activate the acceptance relation. Observed wear activates the need for care. Its experience of returning a clean entrusted tool supports the compatibility of care and release. An unresolved `care_vs_release` tension can thus become a retained composition without deleting its earlier state.

A novice with accurate current tool facts selects an inspection before trying the return. Actual inspection permits that bounded attempt. Only the actor's own completed care work and subsequent successful return can establish the practiced composition. Instruction can make all three relations available without granting practice. A supplied graph and an easy clean return alone do not satisfy the practice rule.

On a new tool demand, an actor retaining the complete practiced composition can choose return without another exploratory inspection. The chosen action still faces the world's actual physical preconditions. The rule remains a finite learning hypothesis. It does not establish general psychological mastery, rationality of every extra inspection, resistance to correction, or a self-maintaining distortion.

Only affected semantic observations enqueue conceptual work. Ordinary timestamps, receipt messages and unrelated ticks do not wake the conceptual processor. Integration and access incur Model A work, can pause without publication, and resume from the journal. Current policy decisions cite the conceptual state's origin as causal evidence.

## Content and supply

A teacher must have actual retained practice before sending an authenticated conceptual lesson or supplying guidance. Both operations pay the supplier's processing costs and require a permitted directed channel. Static content then requires separate learner integration; it grants relations, not practice. Ordinary arbitrary testimony cannot masquerade as this authenticated path.

Live supply here means completed, item-scoped guidance that is available to the recipient at decision time. It is not an unlimited subsidy, the helper performing the learner's physical action, or a full real-time scaffolding theory. Revocation delivers a recipient-visible withdrawal notice. Revocation before selection removes the shortcut; a later withdrawal does not undo work already selected or completed. A successful supplied trial must still include the learner's own actual care and return, and a separate held-out trial after removal checks independence.

## Checkpoints and compatibility

`ConceptualWorld.checkpoint()` serializes configuration, the conceptual reference seal and one typed journal. All indexes, pending queues, remembered inspections, care witnesses and supply availability rebuild by exact replay. Rehashed invented conceptual mastery is rejected by replay comparison.

`ConceptualWorld.import_r14(old_text)` first validates the R14 journal under its original policy and preserves every historical transaction. Thereafter, the integration uses the new boundary. It does not reinterpret old paid work as having used the new architecture. The migration prefix remains recorded for future restores. New partial conceptual work and detail queries have their own continuation controls.

Historical hashes are preserved. Two inherited byte-preservation assertions have explicit new before/after checks for `memory.py` and `memory_records.py`; their originals and initial failures are retained. No inherited behavioral acceptance predicate or frozen R12 acceptance declaration is weakened.

## Remaining limits

The concept grammar, relations, operator targets and exploration policy are supplied. The conceptual learner is primarily additive within this small domain; arbitrary contradictory archetypal interpretations, general belief reorganization, all-rank semantics, partner-generated concept definitions and automatic canonical version migration remain open. The symbolic R13 meaning experiment is preserved as an older API, not recertified as the final archetypal theory.

Current detail selection still enumerates its requested current catalogue. The journal, exact historical revisions and checkpoint/replay costs grow. A constant-cost lookup against inactive history is not proof of constant total ticking or population cost. Conceptual knowledge here is not a Shell: R15 must still generate maintained distortion, recurring costs and material/carrier consequences.
