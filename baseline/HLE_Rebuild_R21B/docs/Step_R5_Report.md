# HLE new build Step R5 report

16 September 2026 · Rebuild release 0.5.0

**R5 is complete within the declared small-world acceptance gate. Progress: 5/10 milestones; five actionable milestones remain. Next: R6 — Socion exchanges among independently adapting holons.** No milestone split was needed.

## What changed

The engine now assesses IDEA return, error, Shell candidates and retained correction using the exact R4 memory, observation, work and action records. `AssessedWorld` extends the existing world and uses its single journal. Assessments do not deliver answers or verdicts to participants.

Four outcomes remain separate: factual agreement, identity return, path conditions and retained capacity. The assessor declares the state/domain, features, operation sequence, resources, path constraints and comparison before engagement. Missing, unavailable and incomplete tests remain unassessed. A failed return stays in the broader claim even when a later trial succeeds.

The IDEA phase grid records explicit contrast and structural predicates. Shared-perspective WE predicates remain unassessed in R5. General ALIGN closure is unassessed for the growing simulation, or failed when an observed return supplies a counterexample. A separate finite-table utility checks the Canon’s Equation 17 exhaustively before applying its composition theorem; the Canon’s three-state counterexample passes once and fails on repetition.

Candidate assessment uses demand, interpretable opportunity, resource sufficiency, actual work and recurrence. Stable error is not automatically a Shell. No false-belief tax was added: energy and time are spent only by actual processing, reconstruction and action.

## Demonstrated results

The integrated demonstration has 36 journal transactions. Its first declared circuit returns the selected ownership content and Crux perspective, so identity return is **established**. An intermediate ownership discrepancy remains visible and violates the predeclared no-discrepancy condition, so path conditions are **failed**. Its 55 paid work units remain recorded. Returning to the original endpoint does not erase the path.

The learned checking procedure then survives and is used on the original box and a held-out tool. Matched controls start with the same history, the same prediction and 3,794 energy/time quanta before the selected recall:

| Demand | Procedure omitted | Procedure recalled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original object | Transfer attempted; fails. | Inspects; discovers the changed owner and withholds the invalid transfer. |
| Held-out object | Transfer attempted; fails. | Inspects; discovers the changed owner and withholds the invalid transfer. |

This demonstrates reusable checking within the supplied ownership/action grammar. It does not establish unrestricted concept formation or transfer between arbitrary world semantics.

Matched quiet trajectories distinguish rest from foreclosure only when named demand, direct interpretable evidence and an affordable movement are supplied. The compensation fixture requires recurring paid reconstruction of a still-false account despite direct counterevidence. Ignorance, unavailable movement and insufficient energy/time remain distinct.

A candidate lifecycle remains unresolved after a quiet interval or insufficient retained evidence. In the correction fixture it moves from `foreclosure_candidate` through two `candidate_unresolved` reports to `cleared_in_tested_demands`, after completed corrective demands and retained checking use on both objects.

The projection/re-ownership fixture produces a complete addressable chain: a Theorize account → generated owned material → explicit unowned treatment and carrier selection → a directed attribution message → delivered testimony → owned revision 3 → renewed expression that withholds external attribution. An ordinary ownership error cannot substitute for this chain.

**Foreclosure, compensation and material-treatment choices are deliberately injected detector fixtures. They are not evidence of autonomously generated Shells.** Their correction and clearance results are bounded to the tested fixture policies and demands. Independent partner adaptation remains R6.

Numerical prices, opportunity/recurrence thresholds, identity features, and carrier selection are implementation choices.
