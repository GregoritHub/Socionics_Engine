timing, storage and continuation gates. Seven boundary controls also pass their

delivered archive; exact source hashes and release-gate results are in the evidence.

physical, memory, processing, assessment, composition, language and organization

The declaration was saved before the first R10 workload execution. No workload,

## Independent verification and replay

The new offline oracle reconstructs balances, ownership, linked action outcomes,
exact locally received consent and practice summaries from configuration and
journal records, independently of runtime selection/guard/index helpers. All
comparisons match. Negative tests reject missing paid consent, unearned proposals
and corrupted maintained practice counts. This is a bounded oracle, not an
independent implementation of every semantic or psychological rule. Inherited
memory, processing, composition and assessment references remain required tests.

Every main case restores after its second lifecycle and continues with the exact
remaining commands, matching each transaction and the final checkpoint bytes.
Exhaustion checkpoints restore unchanged. Python hash seeds 0, 1 and 77 give the
same checkpoint for the declared three-cycle four-actor workload; these are
separate from the workload-seed variations.

## Efficiency and growth

Fixed-active formulation is measured after 0, 1,000 and 10,000 inactive ticks or
unrelated paid inspection receipts. Every condition retains **one candidate visit
and ten charged units**. Development medians were approximately 0.22–0.26 ms;
the evidence records the final run's exact timings and environment separately.

| Active extent | Candidate visits | Charged formulation units |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 candidate, 3 members | 1 | 10 |
| 4 candidates, 3 members | 4 | 22 |
| 16 candidates, 3 members | 16 | 70 |
| 64 candidates, 3 members | 64 | 262 |
| 1 candidate, 15 members | 1 | 34 |
| 1 candidate, 31 members | 1 | 66 |
| 1 candidate, 63 members | 1 | 130 |

Charges match the declared candidate/member/sequence/access formula. The 63-member
profile measures formulation only; full lifecycle populations stop at 16 actors.
All-member consent uses quadratic communication.

The unchanged gates require fixed-active median no greater than the larger of
three times baseline or 2 ms, and p95 at most 10 ms. Growing-active limits are
5 ms plus 0.25 ms per candidate/member. Sustained paid-command p95 must be at most
20 ms, with a separate first/last-quarter comparison. All pass. Offline evaluator
time is measured separately from participant execution.

Storage and checkpoint work remain significant. The development 16-actor,
transfer-only, ample-resource seed-17 run has **9,901 events and 96,032,971
uncompressed checkpoint bytes**, about 8.1 seconds encoding and 13.2 seconds
restoration/continuation on that run. Final-run timings are separately recorded.
Full journals are compressed for delivery; their retained information and replay
cost remain. Archive gates allow 20,000 bytes/event, 2 ms/event plus one second
for encoding, and 5 ms/event plus one second for replay.

At main-panel lifecycle boundaries, organizational, language, composition,
reception and physical jobs are finished. Historical records and indexes grow.
Direct inspection notifications also accumulate for the **separate, unscheduled
R6 controller**, explicitly counted in final diagnostics. This does not establish
a globally empty queue, backpressure, bounded memory or simultaneous autonomous
operation of every controller. The tested selector does not rescan inactive history.
