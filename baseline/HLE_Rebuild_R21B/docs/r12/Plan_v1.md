## Definitions that govern completion

**First-order individuation.** With TIM and Model A dimensions fixed, a holon acquires usable access to its complementary valued domain, returns carrying that capacity, and continues to use it on consequential demands. The native capacity remains usable. A helper can scaffold acquisition, but the helper performing the missing work cannot establish the learner's retention.

**Full individuation in the engine.** First-order crossing participates in the wider organization of identity, relationship, action, and shared structure. Material implicated in the Hero, Child, Parent, and Spirit complexes can become owned, differentiated, and usable. The relevant four-perspective circuits can complete without persistent Shell signs, and the result can serve as a constituent of further coherent organization. There is no mandatory universal route sequence or final personality state.

**Shell.** A demand-conditioned, self-maintaining distortion of a realized movement. A candidate requires a traceable mechanism and recurring evidence. Ignorance, an unavailable operation, inability to afford it, justified refusal, rest, and ordinary unsuccessful exploration need their own outcomes.

**Shell clearance.** An established Shell ceases to maintain the relevant distortion under renewed, comparable or greater demand while useful material and acquired capacity remain available. The verdict belongs to a material lineage, capacity revision, demand family, resource envelope, and observation window. New contradictory evidence can reopen it.

**Recursive closure.** A retained organization becomes a coherent constituent of a larger organization that meets an otherwise unmet demand. Altitude is counted from established coherent closures. An additional wrapper, a graph cycle, a success counter, or a changed label cannot increment it.

These definitions operationalize the supplied framework. The detailed selection policies, learning grammar, evidence thresholds, and numerical work prices remain implementation choices until their scope and evidence are declared.

## Structural commitments

1. Preserve the missing primitive horizontal connections. The adopted function graph has F: I–WE, T: IT–ITS, N: I–ITS, and S: IT–WE. It has no primitive I–IT or WE–ITS function edge. Realize a developmental crossing through acquired organization and lawful operations. The formal Crux routes Express, Embody, Institutionalize, and Educate already name within-row transformations; their presence in the route table does not supply an instantaneous function edge or acquired competence.
2. Keep TIM, active information element, content aspect, Model A position, Crux perspective, retained capacity, and altitude separate. Retention changes what the holon can use under demand. It does not change the 4–3–2–1–1–2–3–4 dimensionality sequence.
3. Use one causal history. Crux, OIG, memory, material ownership, resource costs, and assessment are linked views of that history. They must not become unrelated simulations that happen to share labels.
4. Preserve personal meaning. Card cues, contextual associations, and their revisions belong to the holon. The Fool's Memory structure does not require every holon to adopt the author's personal interpretations.
5. Keep three uses of identity distinct: persistent actor/material identity, the manual's Id/Identity register, and the IDEA feature map κ. Their relationship must be stated explicitly where used.
6. Charge actual work. Defensive reconstruction, suppression, carrier switching, checking, learning, and practice consume resources when they occur. A false belief does not receive an arbitrary energy penalty merely because the evaluator knows it is false. A maintained false account can be relatively stable while its ongoing maintenance consumes resources.
7. Let participant decisions use permitted observations, owned memory, and paid processing. Simulator Truth and evaluator verdicts remain outside the participant interface. A holon can make its own fallible assessment through the same information restrictions.
8. Preserve epistemic grades. Historical kernel findings remain historical until reproduced on the new implementation. Mathematical clock reversal constrains transformations; it does not derive a numerical psychological work price.

### R17 — Convert material through practice and re-ownership

Implement the source cycle as operations with consequences:

1. **Release:** preserve access to active material without immediately replacing it with a defensive story or action.
2. **Leadership:** obtain and organize the context needed to reconsider placement and test alternative use.
3. **Reversion:** allow an unfinished reorganization and revise it from consequences without forcing premature closure.
4. **Re-ownership:** retain the resulting capacity, bring it back into ordinary functioning, and put it to work under renewed demand.

The sources call these phases CAPE. A phase is established by its work and results. The runtime should permit interrupted, partial, repeated, or unsuccessful attempts; advancing a four-state counter cannot manufacture integration.

Reproduce the source-defined compensation/individuation relationships and lap constraints where used. Re-aiming from the n-image toward the cp-image supplies a structural target. The participant still has to acquire, process, act, and retain content. Numerical cost follows the elected work model and actual operations.

**Pass:** a generated compensatory episode changes because the holon processes and reuses its material. Removing access, practice, or retention prevents the corresponding achievement. A repair that only deletes material fails. Historical provenance stays recoverable, and new consequential behavior shows what has changed. The retained capacity changes response under load without an `if altitude` shortcut or a directly assigned “developed” flag.

### R19 — Establish clearance and reopen it on recurrence

Assess each established Shell against the same material lineage and relevant demand. Record the capacity and evidence revisions supporting the verdict. A later invalidation, loss of access, repeated defensive pattern, carrier displacement, or failed consequential return must make the affected verdict unresolved or recurrent. Preserve the earlier qualified result in history.

Proposed acceptance protocol, to be frozen in R12:

- Establish maintenance across at least three eligible renewed engagements before attempting to claim its clearance. Three is an engineering threshold, not a theorem; retain sensitivity results at two and five engagements.
- Reapply the original demand after integration, then use twelve held-out challenges across at least three demand families. Keep the original twelve selection tasks as a regression panel, separately.
- Include equal demand, an explicitly comparable increase, a different context/partner, delay/interruption, and changed ownership or testimony where relevant. An incomparable demand extends the evidence family; it is not automatically “greater.”
- Require successful consequential handling under each declared positive case, retained native and complementary capacities, and no persistent target Shell sign within the declared window. Valid refusal or deferral can be the correct response only when specified by the task's conditions.
- On matched equal-demand comparisons, measure reduced defensive work, recurrent cancellation, or blocked opportunity where these existed at baseline. Do not require every ordinary computational cost or every benign cancellation to vanish. Foreclosure relief is demonstrated by resumed conductive action, even if total work rises from inactivity.
- Test the same material against at least two potential carriers. A quieter first relationship accompanied by displaced attribution elsewhere fails clearance.
- Continue under sustained eligible demand. Support withdrawal removes the helper doing the target work while preserving comparable information and resource opportunity; turning the world into a starvation fixture does not test retained competence.

**Pass:** the positive cases clear, every suppression/displacement/stale-evidence control fails to clear, and a renewed failure reopens the relevant current verdict. Mere time passage cannot clear a Shell. Deleting memories, reducing the demand, resetting the actor, or changing κ after a failure cannot satisfy the original claim.

This is the first complete individual development-and-clearance checkpoint. Report the particular Shells cleared, unresolved, and recurrent, with their demands and horizons.
