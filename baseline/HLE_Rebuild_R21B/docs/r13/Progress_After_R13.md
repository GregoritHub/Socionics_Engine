## Immediate R14 work

Connect actual world consequences to persistent problems. A fulfilled transfer may create a commitment, coordination may create a dependency, and changed ownership may invalidate a prior procedure. The participant must discover the problem through permitted observations and retained expectations, not receive a simulator Truth answer or a “develop now” condition label.

Use R13’s contextual capacity and personal-meaning interfaces to choose among investigation, recall, action, help, teaching, waiting, refusal and revision. Keep need, discrepancy, pressure and boredom distinct. A quiet satisfied state is a legitimate outcome; it must not be made busy to manufacture development.

R14’s gate remains the original one: demonstrate at least two different persistent consequences producing different subsequent demands; remove the consequence and its demand disappears; changing observations changes the available response; an observation-matched hidden-Truth change cannot change the choice. Honest refusal, unaffordable work, waiting, search exhaustion and unresolved problems must not automatically become Shells.

## Handoff from R13

Use `hle.developmental.DevelopmentalWorld`, not the pinned adapter. Content inference, contextual access, current capacity and personally meaningful revision now share the runtime journal and wallets. Checkpoints include unfinished processing and preserve the exact rest of execution. Keep future generated demands and policy state equally versioned and replayable.

The existing `hle.development_demo` tasks and history sequences are **test/controller scaffolding**, not participant-generated demands. R14 must replace that source of work where it claims generation, rather than relabeling the R13 panels as endogenous.
