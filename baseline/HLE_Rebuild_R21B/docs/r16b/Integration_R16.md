## Consequential workflow

1. A clean due loan with practiced capacity triggers paid holding and interpretation of the actor's own current terms. A stable material root references its conceptual source and particular observation; subsequent loans have distinct engagement identities.
2. The actor compares the cost of separating historical practice from current authority with a short summary. The cheap summary can retain the historical universal prerequisite before that distinction. The alternative actually revises the personal conceptual relation.
3. Paid integration produces two addressable components. Componentwise checking can pronounce them jointly complete despite their incompatible release requirements. Cross-consistency checking instead retains the disagreement as open.
4. Two separately paid messages consume those components. Both are delivered to the lender about the same actor, item and loan. Their requirements can conflict. They are participant statements, not facts inserted into the shared Truth record.
5. The peer independently compares the actual delivered messages. A completed but contradictory account causes a reply requiring two source-specific justifications instead of one coherent release account. The demand increase preserves actor, loan, context and requirement dimension.
6. After interpreting that reply, the participant pays to construct either an exception wrapper or a source-indexed scaffold. The latter actually removes the inappropriate conceptual prerequisite. Using the structure sets the release permission that determines whether source correction remains necessary.
7. A paid maintaining guard can consume the selected source-separation opportunity; increasing its relative work cost selects actual source separation instead. The former keeps the account unchanged, the latter changes the concept and later release behavior.
8. Ordinary R15 release continues. The maintained account chooses a carrier, pays for confirmation and assimilation, and returns the tool. Corrected accounts return directly. Exact physical ownership and conceptual coherence remain separate.

Particular task records remain outside Fool's Memory. The conceptual reference and rank meanings are unchanged; personal conceptual relations and tensions can change. A held account is an explicit material record, not a duplicate store of all facts.

## Replay and accounting

`reconciliation_reference.py` independently folds raw transactions. It reconstructs source references, demand changes, component actions, guard/correction behavior, all twelve OIG paths, physical ownership and work. It does not call participant selection, the live adapter, the five detector predicates or the content projection. Its comparison wrapper consumes completed reports only after the independent fold has finished. R15's independent wallet evaluator also checks all new charges.

Jobs publish nothing until funded. A zero-spend retry is deferred even when earlier work was partly paid. Changed owned inputs fail without publication; processing ownership rejects overlapping commands. Checkpoint restore regenerates every transaction from its command and rejects rehashed fabricated outputs, movements and parent schemas. `ReconciliationWorld.import_r15` verifies and preserves ordinary R15/R16A histories; migration-bearing older R14.5 imports remain outside this new checkpoint format, while their original runtime is retained.
