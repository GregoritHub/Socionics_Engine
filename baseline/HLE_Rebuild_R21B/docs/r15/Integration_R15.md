## One causal process

`CompensationWorld` subclasses `ConceptualWorld`. It preserves exact observation storage, factual memory, fixed Model A profiles, Crux/Fold routes, workshop actions, generated return demands and conceptual learning. New records live in the same typed journal. No evaluator verdict selects behavior.

The harness introduces ordinary raw workpieces and available tools. Actors independently request, lend, use, care for and return them. Some training loans genuinely require peer confirmation; that term is explicitly observed. Physical return enforces it even if a caller tries to bypass it. A partner's paid inspection and authenticated response satisfy the actual requirement.

On the learner's own successful confirmed return, the conceptual learner generalizes `release requires external_confirmation`. This is a personal ConceptState relation, separate from the immutable reference. It is absent at genesis. The harness supplies no false observation, prerequisite, diagnosis, Shell flag or defense sequence. Direct-return history does not induce the relation.

Later loans explicitly permit independent release. The learner has accurate details, practiced care/release, a clean due tool and sufficient resources. At a participant-selected return, paid consideration compares owned current terms with the learned prerequisite. A mismatch generates TensionMaterial and MaterialTreatment(HOLD). The material records the conceptual constraint, stable address, original concept and original evidence references.

The supplied alternatives are direct release when no unmet prerequisite exists; confirmation from an accessible peer; and revision of the learner's extra prerequisite. Confirmation is estimated at 2 + the peer's quoted content units; revision at 1 + revision_unit × support count. These estimates are not complete predicted partner Model A costs. Actual charged work is separately recorded for consideration, enactment, review and response assimilation. The heuristic is not a global resource optimum.

With enough support, immediate accommodation wins. Paid enactment republishes the conceptual relation and unresolved conflict, treats the material as externally carried, and sends a real request to the selected participant. The request expresses the learner's prerequisite, not world truth. A recipient pays for interpretation and physical inspection and may confirm or refuse, subject to its own boundary, inspection repertoire, resources and discovered needs. An optional confirmation explicitly states that independent release is permitted. Paid assimilation preserves that counterevidence while retaining the extra relation. The actor then returns the clean tool correctly. Another confirmed success reinforces the same generalized prerequisite.

This is a deliberately bounded self-maintenance hypothesis. The experiment establishes its implemented behavior, not that the hypothesis uniquely follows from the papers or explains human cognition.

## Material, access and ownership

TensionMaterial fixes the originating actor/event, conceptual constraint and initial evidence. It is distinct from the physical tool and its owner. Its original address and evidence remain available in the owner's exact record space. MaterialTreatment uses the frozen R12 contract, with linked HOLD, EXTERNALIZE and REOWN revisions, selection evidence, actual work references and observed consequences.

Enactment requires the exact owned decision and current treatment. Selection uses owned service-offer observations and already delivered refusals. The cheaper third party can carry the request while the lender remains the physical return recipient. Swapping the quotes changes the carrier. A refusal excludes that peer from the current search. The harness does not supply a target sequence.

After both peers refuse, a fresh comparison leaves revision available. The actor pays to reprocess the supports, removes its unnecessary conceptual prerequisite, and records REOWN with itself as carrier. It then returns independently. Original material and all previous treatments survive. This establishes R15.2's local ownership transition; it does not establish R17's full conversion/practice/retention controls or R19 clearance.

No deletion of access or corruption of particular facts creates the pattern. Externalization changes responsibility for a conceptual constraint while preserving provenance. Arbitrary suppression and the remaining Shell-sign mechanisms are still open.

## Isolation, continuation and accounting

`select_release` receives only a detached ReleaseView. It has no world, Truth, global journal, foreign private memory or assessment interface. Exact terms, concept, supporting observations and carrier evidence resolve through owned indexes. A paid review is a declared physical sensing boundary, like workshop inspection. A hidden world change leaves an unchanged owned view and selection unchanged until a permitted consequence supplies new information.

Jobs contain immutable inputs, actual debits, explicit Crux movements and Model A routes. Unfinished jobs exclusively own personal processing. No usable material, treatment, concept revision, request or response appears before full funding. Current evidence is rechecked; new owned evidence can fail a stale job without refund or publication. Used decisions, requests, responses and command IDs cannot be reused as fresh work. A pending request cannot be overwritten by another consideration for the same loan.

Typed restore reexecutes commands and compares every produced transaction. Rehashed invented origins, carriers and conceptual outputs fail. R14.5 import validates the old checkpoint under its original behavior and preserves its transactions. Paid offers appear afterward. Old work is not credited as new conceptual processing.

The offline evaluator folds world changes, wallet credits/debits, job payments, personal relations, material predecessors and carrier consequences from transactions. It does not call selection or consult derived runtime material indexes. Endpoint correctness, conceptual distortion, recurring work and treatment history are separate witnesses; none feeds the participant.

## Remaining limits

The repertoire, loan rule, message grammar and learning trigger are supplied. Arbitrary concepts, defenses, language and complete archetypal semantics are not generated. The primary service offers are public. Wider policies for changing prices, announcing renewed availability and reopening an exhausted genuine requirement are not implemented here.

Inactive tick history is not walked during consideration. Relevant retained supports and the factual catalogue still affect processing costs; journals and checkpoints grow. Sixteen-type routing coverage is not full individuation, all-complex coverage, five-sign/OIG agreement or the frozen R21 population evaluation.
