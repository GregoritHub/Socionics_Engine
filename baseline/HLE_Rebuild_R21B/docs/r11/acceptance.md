## Processing ownership and partial work

R4, R6 reception, R8 and R9 cannot concurrently mutate one actor's activation. An unfinished semantic job owns the state by family and task, independent of string collisions. Other actors and R2/R3/R7 work remain schedulable. Resume retains route, starting-state reference, original candidate, required extent and paid units. Only fully paid hops advance activation. No usable semantic output, consent, lexeme or executable plan is installed before all route and content work completes. Every funded retry checks current permitted evidence; invalidated jobs fail without publishing their candidate and release ownership. Explicit semantic cancellation publishes a failed terminal receipt, preserves costs/activation and releases ownership. There is no work refund or replacement output. R4/reception cancellation is outside this milestone.

Supplied lifecycle opportunities and finite procedure grammar remain.
