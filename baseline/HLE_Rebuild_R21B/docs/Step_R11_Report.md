# HLE new build: R11 report

17 September 2026 · release 1.1.0

**R11 is complete within its fresh declared scope. Model A now routes and prices actual language and organization work, updates activation, and affects following operations and message reception.** Ne remains the default starting element for all 16 types. R1-R10 retain their historical acceptance; the scoped rebuild now has **11/11 milestones complete and zero remaining assigned R milestones**.

## What changed

All four language operations (Learn, Produce, Interpret, Intend) and eight organization operations (Formulate, ReviewTerms, Ratify, Join, Attend, Dispute, Leave, Perform) use one paid processing mechanism. The existing semantic operations still derive their results from owned evidence and acquired meanings. They now carry immutable Model A routes, original content extent, target-dependent content prices, exact partial payment and processing-state provenance. Fully funded outputs retain their original downstream consequences: lexemes, understood requests, commitments, consent, executable plans and physical actions.

R4, reception, language and organization share one actor's processing state safely. Conflicting jobs reject while another job owns processing. Explicit semantic cancellation retains costs and attained activation, publishes no candidate and releases ownership. Changed local evidence can invalidate a partial job; it fails before additional payment and installs no output. R4 must make a valid return from ITS/IT before these local I-perspective operations can begin. No perspective reset was introduced.

The original operation mappings and cost choices were declared before implementation acceptance in `docs/r11/acceptance.md`. They are **modeling hypotheses**, not deductions establishing psychological truth. Their local formal boundary is I -> I; an organization packet does not itself establish a collective WE perspective. The R4 cube/support selector and the funded-hop routine are shared. Numerical costs are abstract energy/time quanta, not CPU instructions or biological measurements.

## Direct answer to the probe

The unchanged original probe still confirms that all types default to Ne. It now stops at its P2 assertion because the old observation is false:

| Probe boundary | Alice | Bob | Cara | Dara |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| After acquired language training | Ti | Fe | Fe | Ne |
| After Formulate/Review/Ratify/Perform | Te | Fi | Fi | Ne |

Dara has not participated in those operations and remains at the initial element. The probe's later clauses do not execute after P2; their old results are not claimed as new results.

In the separate matched emission control, the same IEE sender and receiver both initially start at Ne and exchange identical speech. Enabled routing makes Produce end at Fe; the receiver takes Ne -> Se -> Ni -> Fe for **7 units**. With semantic routing ablated, Produce leaves Ne; reception takes Ne -> Si -> Ne for **6 units**. Receiver initial activation, speech and profiles are held fixed. Thus this difference is caused by actual semantic processing, not changed initialization or changed message meaning.

## Historical expectations and retained failures

Of the 340 inherited tests, 337 retain their original assertions. Two tests that formerly allowed competing semantic operations now check exclusive ownership, explicit cancellation and retained payment; separate new tests preserve stale-evidence failure coverage. One byte-preservation test allows the documented shared-hop refactor in processing.py, metabolism.py and socion.py. Original hashes and R10 files are retained. Thirteen new tests bring the suite to 353.

Construction logs retain the two old concurrency errors, the historical byte-hash mismatch, and three corrected test-fixture errors. The fixture fixes used unique command IDs and an actually different tampered state. The unchanged probe's intentional P2 failure is separately identified. No acceptance threshold, target mapping or behavioral failure was concealed or relaxed.

The final source verifier checks all modules used by evaluation, the exact workload/controls/oracles and positional fixture, plus final runtime/test hashes. The command-line demonstration text and release/report tools were finalized after panel execution; they do not execute in that panel. This provenance boundary is explicit in `final-release-check.json`.

## What remains outside this completion

The release still uses finite acquired symbolic meanings and a finite ownership-procedure grammar with supplied lifecycle opportunities. It does not establish spontaneous institutional motivation, unrestricted language, generated Shell emergence/clearance, psychological calibration, general recursive Cross closure, a new Id/Identity cycle or an open-world game. The new mappings and prices remain open to better specified alternatives and empirical testing.

R11 language/organization checkpoints use new schemas. Automatic migration of R10 live sessions is not supplied; the original R10 source archive is included for replay. R2-R7 checkpoint contracts remain unchanged. See `Progress_After_R11.md` for the exact scoped step status.
