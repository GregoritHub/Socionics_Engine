# R17 conversion, practice and reownership

`ConversionWorld` subclasses `ReconciliationWorld`. It continues the same world,
actors, Model A processing, observations, conceptual references, R15 material and
treatment history, R16 account history, physical workshop and paid event journal.
No earlier checkpoint or assessment is silently relabeled as developed.

## Source authority

The frozen R12 plan (§R17), operator registry and acceptance gates R17.1/R17.2
remain unchanged. All nine newly supplied source files match the packaged R12
originals byte for byte (`Source_Input_Check.json`). *A Fresh Perspective on
Disorder*, P0062–P0067, supplies transport, retention, the n/cp distinction and
the directive/lap constraint. Its interpretation and historical engine claims
keep their original grades. R12 Source Decisions D14 and D18 explicitly require
new behavioral evidence; the historical A23 claim is not substituted for it.

The finite hypothesis grammar, work prices, two-branch practice threshold,
availability-dependent fallback and failed-return inspection rule are declared
implementation hypotheses. The user/source architecture remains intact:
particulars are indexed observations, conceptual tension references them, and
shared rank/suit/concept meanings are immutable.

## Consequential operations

**Release** is a paid opening over an already generated R15 material root and
its R16 account root. It retains the original concept, foreign-carrier treatment
chain and exact observations. It changes current treatment to holding by the
actor, without changing the concept or granting a capability. The demonstration
explicitly offers this opportunity; spontaneous initiation is not claimed.

**Leadership/context acquisition** pays to retrieve and organize the actor's
own observation references. The learner distinguishes a confirmation that
happened from confirmation required by current accepted terms. Contrasting
required and optional terms eliminate both constant alternatives in an elected
three-hypothesis grammar. Ambiguous context cannot publish a selected rule.

**Reversion** creates a provisional conditional procedure. It is usable only
within the open study, and confers no retained ordinary capacity. Actual loans
require native use, care, return selection and physical return. Paid reflection
reads the actor's own consequence and revises the candidate's supported domain.
One successful optional return leaves the required branch open. A required
return must actually receive confirmation. Separate successful loan consequences
are necessary for both branches.

A changed physical precondition can make a real practice attempt fail. The
failure remains in the candidate and journal, earns no branch credit, and adds
an executable fresh-own-inspection guard. The actor can pay for cleaning,
inspection and another attempt. That learned guard is retained and executed on
later use. Missing native operations leave recovery unresolved. Pauses preserve
the unfinished study, and partial work publishes no candidate or capacity.

**Reownership** retains the practiced procedure, its original material,
acquisition debits and physical practice observations. A real `REOWN` treatment
continues the same historical chain. The current personal concept becomes
conditional on accepted terms. The old universal relation is recoverable in
its original revision, and all former carriers remain in historical records.

Ordinary functioning requires paid recall of a current retained capacity plus
access to the original material. The resulting application is scoped to the
current exact loan and owned terms. The existing account/release operators then
consume it: optional loans return directly, without the earlier account-message
round trip, while genuinely required loans
still obtain confirmation. A helper does not supply the optional answer or act
for the learner. Without usable retained processing, the preserved earlier habit
is the available fallback; a bare conditional concept label is insufficient.

## Model A and structural limits

The inherited exact checks retain nEn=E and cpEcp=E⁻¹ for every seat. Paid
Model A work routing is not silently equated with a four-phase lap traversal.
Accepting conversion work targets cp(1)=5 rather than n(1)=3. Both preserve the
directive coordinate. Producing work targets E(5)=6; a portage is never treated
as an instantaneous accepting-to-producing transformation. Model A determines
the actual routed processing and prices, and completed units determine active
processing state. The assignments are finite hypotheses. These operations do
not establish all sixteen types' complementary circuits or the R18 complexes.

## Controls and integrity

The panel first generates three gated historical returns and three optional
maintained engagements. Every condition then receives the same two practice
opportunities and four later loans. It separately removes material access,
practice, and retention. A second control restricts access or withdraws capacity
after successful acquisition. Live material deletion is a fault-injection test:
it cannot license independent use, and is not an implemented deletion workflow.

For the main comparison, each actor has exactly 20,000 energy and time units at
the renewal boundary. Histories are replayed under initial allocations that
produce those balances; this manipulation changes resources, not observations
or acquired content. The former cheapest carrier withdraws before renewal,
while a second genuine carrier remains available to every condition. The
held-out item/partner cases remain within the workshop context; no broader
context equivalence or R19 clearance is inferred.

`conversion_reference.py` independently folds raw transactions, reconstructing
work, physical ownership, original material, treatments, context, practice,
capacity and actual uses. It never calls participant inference, selection,
`release_view` or a live monitor. Replay regenerates transactions from commands
and rejects rehashed fabricated outputs. R16 import preserves its old journal
and five-sign history exactly. The R16 observer remains available; historical
positive findings survive later converted behavior.

Current processing uses addressed records. The inactive probe forbids journal
iteration during the next conversion operation after 0, 100 and 1,000 quiet
ticks. Histories, support sets, checkpoints and offline evaluation still grow.
This is not the R21 efficiency or 480-case release gate.

The evidence runner also releases completed world objects before serializing
other checkpoint cases. An offline verification session lost its workspace
connection while several complete histories were retained. Its already saved
full replay and trajectory results survived; the resumable runner verifies the
checkpoint digest and exactly reconstructs all four trajectory reports before
continuing the remaining checks. This changes evaluator memory lifetime, not
participant work or the frozen acceptance criteria.
