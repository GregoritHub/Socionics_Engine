# R9 organization hypothesis and acceptance contract

17 September 2026. All R9 selection, consent, allocation, lifecycle and price laws
below are **implementation choices**. The roadmap requests these capabilities;
it does not derive this particular institution family from Socionics, the Crux,
Fool's Memory or IDEA. Source anchors: preserved roadmap R9 and foundation
sections 1, 3, 5 and 8. Existing source grades remain unchanged. Gate identifiers
G01–G07 deliberately differ from foundation open-question identifiers O01–O07.

## Elected family and generic parts

The small world already supplies ownership, inspection, transfer, messages,
retained evidence, recursive access and acquired ownership meanings. R9 adds
three compositors: responsibility assigned to the current consenting holder;
an inspection prefix followed by one transfer; and allocation to the next member
in canonical member-reference order. Cyclic allocation and the ordering rule are
supplied, not discovered. No particular organization, member cohort, object,
meaning or action sequence is installed in the initial world.

A participant's indexed practice summaries are updated only when its own accepted
R8 interpretation or R9 enactment reaches actual fulfillment, failure or an
inspection-blocked endpoint. The pattern must use one acquired two-argument
ownership concept. All actions must concern that object; inspections may precede
one final transfer. Successful episodes contribute experienced partners. Templates
with fewer than the participant's required demonstrations (default two),
nonpositive `successes - 2*failures`, incompatible current meaning, or excessive
physical cost are ineligible. Selection maximizes that score, then minimizes
cost, with explicit deterministic reference/sequence/token tie breaks. Thus more
than one action sequence can be generated, but this is a finite grammar, not an
arbitrary institutional programming language.

The initial cohort comes from the elected practice's actual successful partners
and the proposer. A revision keeps the prior item, removes members only on
received departure evidence, and adds locally received checked join applicants.
Each other existing member separately checks removals against its received exits.
The host cannot pass ready-made Terms to Formulate. Terms are the operation's
result. Names, training opportunities, communication schedule and opportunities
to formulate/enact remain supplied in this milestone.

## Identity and agreement

Terms contain organization identity, generation, predecessor fingerprint, object,
acquired meaning, action sequence and the exact member cohort. Every field enters
the version fingerprint. Historical terms are immutable. A changed action sequence
or cohort needs fresh consent; votes for another identity or generation do not
apply. This exact-field identity is an elected operational definition, not a proof
of general IDEA identity preservation.

Proposal, review, membership application, agreement, departure and dispute
packets use ordinary directed R2 sends. Wire content contains public terms and
local protocol claims, not private memory or composition addresses. R6 paid Model A
reception must finish before the packet can be read. The channel checks that a
packet originated in an actual committed record owned by its sender. Raw messages
cannot forge another participant's consent or create an unearned local review.
This is an in-simulator authenticity rule, not cryptography or a hostile-Python
sandbox.

Review uses the receiver's own acquired lexical pattern and paid retained access.
Missing meaning, unknown ownership, conflicting evidence, stale access, silent
response policy and excessive declared procedure cost remain distinct reasons
not to agree. A member accepts responsibility only for an offered seat. The current
retained owner must belong to the proposed cohort. This is a retained belief check;
it can be factually wrong. Ratification requires the participant's own accepted
review and one exact accepted vote from every other member, received locally.
Duplicate, missing, refused, mixed-version or stale-head votes cannot activate a
procedure. A newcomer agrees to the new terms; it does not inherit the founder's
private history or silently acquire an office.

Agreement is **local**. A member receiving every vote does not imply that every
other member knows that quorum or has installed the terms. The implementation
makes no atomic distributed-commit or common-knowledge claim. Newcomers check
offered semantics and their own participation, not the full unseen ancestry.

## Recurrence and consequential revision

Perform binds the rule to the acting member and its next consenting successor.
It checks current owned access again and uses real R2 physical actions. Every turn
is a new paid plan. There is no automatic endless scheduler; the supplied harness
presents opportunities. One unfinished enactment per local organization is allowed.
Ordinary world actions outside these linked plans remain available and are not
counted as institutional fulfillment.

An inspection in a procedure has additional consequence: its own fresh contrary
ownership receipt stops the later transfer. Without that inspection, a mistaken
retained claim can reach a paid failed transfer. Neither interpretation nor rule
selection reads the current simulator Truth record.

A dispute must cite the member's own failed or inspection-blocked enactment. It
suspends the author locally; other members suspend only after receiving and
attending to its packet. A disputed sequence becomes ineligible at that local
version. Merely renaming it cannot resolve the dispute. An alternative must first
be present in that participant's successful practice history, and all members must
check and consent to the new version. This intentionally conservative exclusion
rule does not diagnose the ultimate cause of failure, negotiate arbitrary
remedies, or prove the replacement will work in every environment.

Joining requests are advisory until fresh terms and fresh agreement include them.
A departure withdraws the author locally and cancels its outstanding plan. Informed
remaining members suspend pending a revised cohort, or dissolve when fewer than
two remain. Pending linked actions are cancelled on suspension, departure or
replacement; earlier payments and incomplete physical tasks remain recorded.
Uninformed members can remain locally active until a permitted communication
conveys the change. There are no automatic sanctions, external enforcement,
majority voting, shared treasury, economic utility claims or dynamic actor birth.
A newcomer is a previously nonmember actor already present in the world.

## Work and continuation

Every organization work unit charges one energy and one time unit. Formulation
cost is `1 + retained-content count + sum(1 + steps + partners per candidate)`,
plus selected cohort and sequence extents when a proposal is available. Review
and joining cost `2 + retained-content count + steps + members`; ratification
costs `1 + members + received votes`; enactment planning costs
`1 + retained-content count + steps`. Dispute costs `1 + linked physical events`;
attending to a relevant notice costs two; departure costs one. Early unsuccessful
branches charge their stated, inspectable preparation extent. Actual transmission,
reception, memory access and physical actions are charged separately.

Partial jobs retain paid work and their initial candidate. A retry revalidates the
candidate and required extent. A changed candidate produces a paid failure and
cannot silently reuse partial payment for different terms. Exact command retries
are idempotent. Complete checkpoints replay commands and compare every resulting
transaction, rejecting rehashed semantic edits. R1–R8 checkpoint loaders remain
available; automatic migration of live R8 sessions is not implemented.

Practice summaries are maintained at linked action endpoints; ordinary inactive
ticks do not traverse them. Selection visits the requesting participant's active
template summaries and direct lexical addresses. It does not scan the journal or
other participants' memories. Expanding template repertoires, member cohorts,
packets, action indexes and journal history remain real growth costs. Validation,
record construction, index maintenance and protocol serialization are unpriced
simulator overhead and are measured separately from simulated work.

## Gates and declared workload

The pre-evaluation configuration is `docs/acceptance_plan_R9.json`. G01–G07 cover
participant generation; exact paid checked agreement; consequential recurrence;
disputes and revision; succession and dissolution; resource-limited continuation;
and preservation/release verification. The lifecycle uses four declared actors,
three initial members, two training examples, six initial turns, one dispute,
three revised turns, one newcomer, six successor turns and final departures.
Matched controls include no or insufficient history, different action histories,
opaque labels, missing/refused/mixed votes, unknown/conflicting/stale access,
hidden world changes, missing channels and independent resource shortages.

A separate sixteen-case panel varies the second participant across all Model A
types with the other three fixed. Hash seeds 0, 1 and 77 test deterministic replay,
not stochastic behavioral robustness. A descriptive fixed-active profile varies
0 versus 2,000 inactive ticks. R10 population/resource/history/seed evaluation and
sustained efficiency acceptance remain open.
