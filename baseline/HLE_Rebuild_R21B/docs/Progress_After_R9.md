## Boundaries that remain visible

R9's supplied compositors are current-holder responsibility, inspection/transfer sequences and cyclic allocation by canonical member order. Participants select concrete procedures and initial cohorts from successful histories, and revise cohorts using communicated applications and departures. The selection law, minimum support, scoring, individual limits and dispute exclusion rule are modeling choices. R9 does not demonstrate arbitrary institution discovery, sanctions, shared treasuries, English negotiation or spontaneous goals.

Agreement is local: receiving all consents is distinct from knowing that everyone else has received them. Uninformed members do not automatically know another member's dispute or departure. Newcomers check their offered terms and evidence; they do not independently verify unseen complete institutional ancestry.
