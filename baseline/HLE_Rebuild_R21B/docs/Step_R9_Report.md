## What R9 establishes

`OrganizationWorld` extends R8's `LanguageWorld` and the same journal. Participants formulate shared terms from their own consequential practice, independently check proposed responsibilities, exchange exact consent, enact recurring rules, dispute failed use, agree to alternatives, admit a newcomer, continue after the founder leaves, and dissolve when too few participants remain.

The initial world contains no organization. A participant must have at least two successful uses of an eligible acquired-language procedure before it can propose one. Incrementally maintained summaries preserve successes, failures, experienced partners and exact recent episode references. The selection policy favors supported procedures within the participant's cost limits. No-history and one-success controls produce `unresolved`; different successful action histories produce different procedures. Opaque label substitutions preserve behavior.

The implementation supplies a finite grammar: acquired two-argument ownership conditions, a variable inspection prefix, one final transfer, current-holder responsibility and allocation to the next consenting member in canonical reference order. **The cyclic allocation operator is supplied, not invented by the participants.** The actual object, learned meaning, sequence and initial cohort come from participant operations over their own history. Training opportunities, dialogue and lifecycle scheduling are supplied. This establishes participant selection and construction within that family, not unrestricted institutional invention or spontaneous motivation.

All new selection, consent, lifecycle and pricing laws are explicitly labeled implementation choices in `docs/r9/acceptance.md` and the source-linked rule registry. They are not promoted to derived Socionics or IDEA laws.

## Agreement and recurring consequences

Terms preserve an organization identity, generation, predecessor fingerprint, acquired meaning, object, exact action sequence and member cohort. Every field contributes to the version fingerprint. Earlier versions and unsuccessful proposals remain accessible.

A proposal travels through ordinary paid R2 transmission and paid R6 Model A reception. The receiver checks the meaning against its own acquired lexicon and its own paid retained access. It can require repair, remain uncertain, identify conflicting or stale evidence, refuse its proposed participation, or accept. Cost and cohort limits are individual policies. Delivery alone cannot install the organization.

Ratification requires the participant's own accepted review and a distinct accepted vote for the **same exact terms** from every other proposed member. Missing, duplicated, refused, mixed-version and stale-head consents fail. The channel authenticates packets against committed records owned by their senders; raw message content cannot manufacture an unearned consent record.

Agreement is local. Having all votes does not imply that another member has received all votes or installed the terms. Disputes and departures likewise affect uninformed peers only after permitted communication. There is no claim of atomic distributed agreement or common knowledge.

For each new opportunity, a member pays to check its retained evidence and bind the rule to itself and its successor. A supported condition creates an executable plan with real inspection/transfer costs. Missing, contradictory, conflicting, stale or changed meanings cannot grant action. A hidden ownership change leaves the local plan unchanged and can cause actual failure, demonstrating the separation between belief and simulator Truth. A fresh contrary inspection stops the subsequent transfer in procedures that include inspection.

## Observed lifecycle

The main demonstration contains **327 journal transactions** and three successive agreed versions:

| Stage | Observed result |
| --- | --- |
| Formation | Alice selects a transfer procedure and a cohort of Alice, Bob and Cara from two successful acquired-language episodes. Each member independently reviews and consents. |
| Recurrence | Six successive rule-governed transfers fulfill their plans and circulate the shared object. |
| Dispute | An accepted plan encounters changed ownership and incurs a paid failed transfer. Alice raises a failure-backed dispute; informed members suspend. Bob has no eligible alternative yet, so formulation remains `unresolved`. |
| Revision | After two successful inspection-plus-transfer experiences, Bob proposes that alternative. All three members check and consent to generation 2. Three further enactments fulfill. |
| Transmission and succession | Dara independently acquires the shared meaning and requests membership. Alice leaves. Bob proposes generation 3 for Bob, Cara and Dara using received membership and departure evidence. Fresh consent produces six successful successor enactments without Alice. |
| Dissolution | Cara and Dara withdraw. After receiving both departures, Bob's local organization is `dissolved`. Alice remains `withdrawn`. |

A newcomer is a previously nonmember participant already present in the configured world; dynamic actor birth is not implemented. Revision does not erase the original failure or mark its old plan fulfilled. A disputed sequence cannot clear its dispute merely by being renamed. A member can leave; departure, suspension or replacement cancels outstanding local authorization while preserving earlier payments and incomplete tasks.

The inspection alternative is a checked, experienced response within the declared policy. It is not a general causal diagnosis or a proof that inspection resolves every possible organizational failure. There are no sanctions, treasury, majority voting or arbitrary constitutional rules.

## Costs and descriptive efficiency

Organization work charges both energy and time. It includes candidate extent, retained-content extent, membership and action-sequence extent where relevant. Transmission, routed reception, memory access and physical execution charge separately. Partial work retains payment and its proposed result; changed inputs cannot silently reuse the payment for a different outcome. Validation, index maintenance and serialization remain unpriced simulator overhead.

The formulation profile holds one practice template with two successes and two partners constant, excludes one warm-up and measures six operations:

These are descriptive development-run measurements on the recorded Linux/Python environment. Fresh-extraction evidence contains its own rerun. This small profile does not establish sustained scaling across growing repertoires, member cohorts, messages or populations. Journal, packet, action-index and checkpoint growth remain limitations. **R10 sustained-efficiency acceptance is open.**
